**SM MODULE 4 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Strategy Formulation & Implementation — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 4 Ebook + LMS Study Notes Parts 1-3 only*

**MODES —** **Entrepreneurial** = one powerful person, opportunities ·
**adaptive** = reactive · **planning** = analysts, proactive + reactive.

**PROCESS (Ebook) —** Objectives → environment (SWOT) → quantitative
goals (30%) → divisional plans → performance analysis → **selection of
strategy (final)**.

**6 STEPS (SN) —** Strategic mission (time, core values, business
description) → goals → departmental plans → performance analysis (SWOT)
→ action → revise. **SMART**, feedback, drafts.

**LEVELS —** Corporate = **what** (growth, stability, acquisition,
retrenchment) · business = **how to compete** (SBU) · functional = **how
to grow**, daily actions.

**TOOLS —** **ADL** = competitive position (dominant, strong,
favourable, tenable, weak) × industry maturity · **Hofer** = position ×
life-cycle phase · **Shell DPM** = sector profitability × competitive
capability (refines Boston) · **PIMS** = SPI Cambridge, 20 yrs, 2,200+
firms, ~100 items.

**IMPLEMENTATION —** 4th step of SM. **Hrebiniak & Joyce 1984**.
Activities: articulation · validation · communication · monitoring ·
engagement (**ACME**). Slow small steps help.

| **7 stages (SN)**              | **10 failure reasons (SN)**                                              |
|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Clear goals → roles → delegate | Premature upscaling · poor leadership · no succession plan               |
| Execute & monitor → correct    | Overwhelming plan · unrealistic goals · structural focus                 |
| Closure → retrospective        | No empowerment · short-term focus · poor communication · weak monitoring |

**OVERCOME FAILURE —** Support network · **cash flow** · reflect ·
responsibility · feedback · step-by-step. SG: scenario planning,
wargaming, minimise friction.

**LEADERSHIP —** 6 competencies: **anticipate, challenge, interpret,
decide, align, learn**. 3 classes: production, organisational,
strategic. Buy-in needs urgency; implement in phases.

**SBU —** Profit centre; **GE 49 SBUs**; SG: scientific grouping of a
multi-business firm (P&G, LG). Types: product, market, geography,
process, function, hybrid. Not fully independent. Pros: profit,
survival, bookkeeping. Cons: costly, rivalry.
