**SM MODULE 1 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Introduction to Strategies — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 1 Ebook + LMS Study Notes Parts 1-3 only*

**STRATEGY —** Greek **strategos** = "art of the general" (SG: stratus =
army + ago = leading). Plan for long-term goals **under uncertainty**;
links **ends (goals) to means (resources)**. Intended or **emergent**.

**CHANDLER 1962 —** Long-term goals + courses of action + **allocation
of resources**. Studied DuPont, GM, Standard Oil, Sears. (Ebook "Alfred
D", SN "Alfred P".) **Ohmae** = distinguish yourself favourably from
rivals.

**5 TYPES (SN) —** Business · marketing · military · personal
development · problem-solving (root cause) · + environmental (ISO
14001).

**FUNDAMENTALS —** Goal = **where** · scope = **what** · competitive
advantage = **how** · logic = **why**.

| **Horwath 7 phases**                                             | **Years**           |
|------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| Budgetary planning → Corporate planning                          | 1950-60 → 1960-68   |
| Corporate strategy (portfolio) → Industry & competitive analysis | 1968-75 → 1975-85   |
| Internal sourcing (core competencies) → Innovation & execution   | 1985-95 → 1995-2001 |
| Strategic thinking & simplification                              | 2003+               |

**IMPORTANCE —** Ebook: direction · prioritise · accountability ·
communication · decision framework. SN: planning · S&W · skills ·
**resource allocation** · environmental scan.

**PURPOSE OF BUSINESS —** Offer **value** to clients; purpose is
**timeless**. 3 dimensions: economic, social, environmental. **Freeman
1984** stakeholder theory · TBL = profit, people, planet · **SDGs 2015,
17, by 2030**.

**GOALS vs OBJECTIVES —** Goals: broad, long, **not measurable**,
intangible. Objectives: specific, ≤1 year, **SMART**, tangible. (Ebook
also has a reversed view; use LMS.)

**STRATEGIC INTENT —** **Hamel & Prahalad, HBR 1989**; obsession with
winning. Ebook: vision → mission → business definition → objectives. SN
pyramid adds **business model**. **Stretch** = misfit of resources and
aspirations; **leverage** stretches meagre resources; strategy = stretch
not fit.

**VISION —** Top of hierarchy; dream; "where"; 5-10 yrs; core ideology
(values + purpose) + envisioned future.

**MISSION —** Society; "why"; ≤ **250 words**; realistic, distinct, not
static. Mission = external/outsiders; **purpose** = internal/employees.
Slogans: HMT "Timekeepers of the Nation".

**ABELL —** Business definition = **customer groups, customer functions,
alternative technologies** (watch: manual, mechanical, automatic).

**CORE COMPETENCE —** **Prahalad & Hamel, HBR 1990**; skills, not
assets. Tests: many markets · customer benefit · hard to imitate. Honda
engines · Sony miniaturisation · FedEx logistics · Apple design.

> **TRAPS:** *QB: relates goals to means = **strategy** · goals not
> quantifiable = **true** · strategic intent involves **setting
> objectives** · resources vs aspirations misfit = **stretch** ·
> business purpose is not only profit · vision ≠ mission.*
