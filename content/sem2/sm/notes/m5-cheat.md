system. Steps: **benchmark → measure → analyse variance → corrective
action** (lower standards or reformulate).

| Example    | Sales process          | Sales numbers                       |

**CONTROL —** Set standards, measure, correct. Planning and control =
**Siamese twins**. Compliance: COSO, Sarbanes-Oxley, BIS.

**SYNERGY —** Whole \> sum of parts (M&A). Operating: **Tata–Corus 2007,
\$12.02 bn**, 5th largest. Financial: debt capacity, cost of capital.
Marketing: **Hero Honda 1985, 26 yrs**. Kraft–Heinz 2015, \$1.5 bn. SN 7
types: geographies, products, patents, supply chain, IT, R&D, sales.

**STAKEHOLDERS —** Active involvement; power-influence grid; 50 metrics,
**8-10 KPIs**. SN 6 steps: recognise → map (4 quadrants) → expectations
→ scope document → plan → communicate.

**ALLIANCES —** Win-win, **coopetition**. **JV** = child of two parents
(Caradigm: Microsoft + GE 2012). **Equity** = buy shares
(Panasonic–Tesla 2010). **Non-equity** = agreement, most common
(Starbucks + Barnes & Noble). Less binding than a JV.

**PROBLEMS —** Culture/language · uneven power · **lack of trust** ·
goodwill damage · management styles · conflicts. Avoid over-dependence.

**DECISIONS —** Tactical/routine = repetitive, lower level. Strategic =
non-repetitive, big funds, top level.
