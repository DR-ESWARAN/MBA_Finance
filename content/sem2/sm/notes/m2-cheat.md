**SM MODULE 2 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Strategic Management — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Study Notes Parts 1-2 only*

**MEANING —** Art and science of formulating, implementing, evaluating
**cross-functional** decisions. Strategic planning = **formulation
only**; SM term used in **academia**. SM exploits new opportunities;
long-range planning optimises today's trends.

**4 PHASES (Ebook) —** Basic financial planning → forecast-based →
externally oriented → strategic management.

**HISTORY (SN) —** Drucker · **SWOT 1960s (Humphrey)** · Porter Five
Forces 1970s · **RBV 1980s (Barney)** · dynamic capabilities 21st c.
Ebook: Glueck 1984 "stream of decisions and actions".

| **5-stage framework**      | **Question**                    |
|----------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Planning & analysis (SWOT) | Where are we now?               |
| Formulation                | Where do we want to be? (ends)  |
| Alternative selection      | How might we get there? (means) |
| Evaluation                 | Which way is best?              |
| Implementation & control   | How can we ensure arrival?      |

**NATURE (7) —** Process · top-management function · general management
(synergy) · relates to environment (**open system**) · long term ·
flexible · innovative.

**NEED —** Changing environment · employee efficiency · unified decision
making. Importance: **proactive** not reactive; avoid costly mistakes;
reputation.

**STRATEGIST ROLES —** Competitive-advantage officer (M&A) · mobilizer ·
trend forecaster · surveyor (disruption, regulation) · **fund manager**
(portfolio).

**LEVELS —** Board = **supreme authority** · CEO = **most important
strategist**, link to Board · entrepreneur (Drucker) · SBU heads ·
corporate planning staff = **support only** · middle managers =
implementers · EA bread-and-butter = **corporate planner**. BCG consults
on competitive advantage.

**5 TYPES (Ebook) —** Competitive (low-cost, niche) · corporate
(diversified group) · business (unit) · functional (departmental) ·
operating (field).

**12 TYPES (SN) —** Structuralist · differentiation (charge more) ·
**price-skimming** (high → lower) · acquisition · growth · focus (niche)
· **cross-selling** (existing clients) · operational · transformational
(drastic) · sustainability · **diversification** (more activities) ·
retention.

**MGMT LEVELS (SN) —** Top = strategic planning · middle = **tactical**
planning, link · lower = **operational**, reports to middle.

**LIMITATIONS —** Complex · time consuming · difficult to implement ·
needs skilful planning · (SN) poor adaptability · limited to rules · no
short-term benefits.

> **TRAPS:** *QB: not a limitation = **costly process** (likely key) ·
> hard to estimate **competitive responses** · war and winning =
> **strategy** · existing product, new market = **market development** ·
> advantage holds only after imitation fails = **competitive advantage**
> · strategic renewal is not one-time.*
