**HRM MODULE 4 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Job Analysis & Description — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 4 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**CONCEPT —** Gathering and analysing information about the content and
human requirements of jobs and their context. Shows job families and
progression paths.

| **Author**                                  | **Keyword**                                                                           |
|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Wylie**                                   | Anatomy of the job                                                                    |
| **Yoder**                                   | Job = collection of duties, tasks, responsibilities                                   |
| **Jucius** · **Flippo**                     | Studying operations and duties · collecting info on a specific job                    |
| **Blum**                                    | Also the **worker's qualifications**                                                  |
| **Shubin** · **Scott, Clothier & Spriegel** | Distinguish each occupation · critically evaluating operations, duties, relationships |

**9 USES —** HR planning (HR inventory, HRIS) · recruitment & selection
· training · compensation · appraisal · duties info · health & safety ·
job re-engineering · counselling.

**EBOOK PROCESS (5) —** 1 Organisational job analysis (**Terry**: org
charts, flow charts) → 2 Select **representative** jobs → 3 Collect data
→ 4 Job description → 5 Job specification (**last**). Study Notes 5
stages: define purpose → collect → analyse → **validate** → document.

| **Method (Ebook 7)**         | **Remember**                                                              |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Observation**              | Most reliable first-hand; physical, short-cycle jobs; not for mental work |
| Interview · Questionnaire    | Costly, bias, long for managers · many people same job, low cooperation   |
| **Checklist**                | Yes/no ticks; **costly, large firms only**                                |
| Critical incidents · Diaries | Skilled analyst needed · daily record, beats memory lapse                 |
| **Technical conference**     | Supervisors as experts; **lacks accuracy** (job holders absent)           |

**STUDY NOTES 6 METHODS —** Interviews (structured = fixed, unstructured
= flexible) · questionnaires (closed = quantitative, open = qualitative)
· observation · diaries (real-time) · **panels** (HR + SMEs) ·
**existing documentation**.

**PROBLEMS (4) —** No top-management support · one source only ·
untrained job holders · distorted data.

**JOB DESCRIPTION —** **Dessler** (duties, reporting, conditions) ·
**Pigors & Myers** ("pertinent picture") · **Ernest Dale** 6 writing
guidelines · first by-product of job analysis. Components: Ebook 6,
Study Notes 8 (title = "initial gateway", summary, duties, reporting,
qualifications, conditions, compensation, date & approvals).

qualifications**. Personal · physical · mental · social & psychological.
