**HRM MODULE 2 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Human Resource Planning (HRP) — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**CONCEPT —** Continuous, systematic planning ahead for optimum use of
quality employees; best fit people↔jobs; avoids shortage/surplus;
**bridges** org plans and HRM. **Beach**: adequate number of qualified
persons, proper times, satisfaction for individuals. **Decenzo &
Robbins**: right number & kind, right place, right time.

**TYPES (2) —** **Macro** = HR strategic planning / org design &
development, aligned to mission · **Micro** = tactics, right number +
KSA mix per department. Macro drives micro. "Three types" = False.

**OBJECTIVES —** Ebook 12: forecast needs · utilise existing HR ·
availability when required · link to org planning · surplus/shortage ·
deployment info · technology impact · recruitment & training levels ·
labour + housing cost · management development ·
expansion/diversification · productivity. Study Notes 5: forecast ·
align · skill gaps · flexibility · resource use.

| **Factors (Ebook)** | **Items**                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| External (3)        | Economic (suppliers, customers, competitors, globalisation) · Government policies · Technological                                                                                                                 |
| Internal (5)        | Company policies (route to objective) · **Time horizons** (short 6m–1y, long 3–20y; more uncertainty = shorter) · **Type & quality of info** (INTERNAL) · Trade unions · HR systems                               |
| Study Notes         | Internal: workforce, culture, strategic objectives · External: labour market, economy, tech, regulation, globalisation · **Organisational**: strategy alignment, innovation, market position, structure & culture |

| **Process**     | **Steps**                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Ebook (6)       | Environmental scanning → Demand forecast → Supply forecast → Match D&S → Action plan → Monitor, control, feedback |
| Study Notes (8) | Assess workforce → Scan → Forecast → Skill gaps → Strategies → Implement → Monitor & evaluate → Feedback & adapt  |

**FORECASTING (11) —** Bottom-up (lowest level up) · Top-down (market
data down) · **Delphi** (experts) · **NGT** (structured brainstorming) ·
Productivity & staffing ratios · Trend · Regression (dependent from
independent) · Math models · Workload · Group brainstorming (DMAIC:
Analyze, Improve) · Sales force estimate. Study Notes: Quant = trend,
ratio, workload, regression; Qual = expert, Delphi, scenario, focus
groups.

**FLOWS —** In: recruits, promotions, transfers. Out: retirement,
termination, resignation, death, discharge/layoff, downsizing, exit
policy.

**INTERNAL SUPPLY (6) —** Staffing tables · Skills inventories · HRIS ·
Succession planning · **Replacement charts** (key roles + successors) ·
**Renewal analysis** (start at top; vacancy = promoted out + losses).

**EXTERNAL SUPPLY (4) —** Labour markets · Demographic trends ·
**Contingent workforce** (on-demand, not on payroll) · Industry &
occupational trends.

**GAP —** S = D balance · S \< D **shortage** · S \> D **surplus**.
Actions (7): Layoffs · Downsizing · **Attrition** (not replaced) · Early
retirement · Reduced hours · **Outplacement** (dignity) · Hiring (supply
short).

**EVALUATION (Study Notes) —** Alignment with goals · workforce
capability · cost-effectiveness · employee satisfaction. Feedback:
surveys, focus groups, anonymous, executive briefings, loop closure.

> **TRAPS:** *HRP **bridges** (not widens) the gap · info quality is
> **internal** · judging & choosing candidates = **selection**, not
> recruitment · demand forecast not "solely historical" · monitoring
> continues after implementation · HRP is ongoing, for all sizes · LMS
> key: "process begins with forecasting" = True.*
