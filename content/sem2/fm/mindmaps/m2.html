1 Time Value of Money

  • Money now > same money later · earning capacity
  • Linked to time preference
  • Also called present discounted value · DCF = TVM
Study Notes · 5 components

7 Exam Traps

  • FV from PV? Compounding
  • Investment's own return? IRR
  • DCF also called? TVM
  • Why time value? Risk-free return possible today
  • Investment appraisal, not "annual" appraisal
  • QB Q1 key: future value "higher than" (concept: lower)
