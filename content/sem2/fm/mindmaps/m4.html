4 10 Objectives

Profitability · efficiency · solvency (short + long) · reasons for change · inter-firm comparison · forecasts · progress · decision & control · dividend action · granting credit

5 Comparative = Horizontal

  • 2+ periods side by side
  • Absolute, change, %, % change
  • Comparative BS (solvency) · IS (profit)
  • % change = (CY − PY) ÷ PY × 100
Trading: horizontal = comparing this quarter's results with last year's, line by line.

6 Common-size = Vertical

  • Each item as % of a base
  • BS base: total assets / funds = 100
  • IS base: sales = 100
  • Compares different-size firms

7 Exam Traps

  • All BS items as % of total = vertical
  • Vertical is not dynamic
  • Side by side = comparative statement
  • Explaining meaning = interpretation
  • Profitability report = income statement
