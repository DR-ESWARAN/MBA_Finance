**FM MODULE 4 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Financial Statement Analysis — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 4 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**STATEMENTS —** Formal records of activities and position; often
**audited**. Full set (4): balance sheet · income statement · owner's
equity · cash flows. SN: Balance Sheet (position) + P&L (income). Income
statement reviews **profitability**.

**5 FEATURES —** End of accounting period · **facts and opinions** ·
**going concern** · **historical cost** · accountants' **personal
judgement**.

**ANALYSIS —** Critical evaluation for decisions = **analysis**
(methodical classification) + **interpretation** (meaning,
significance). **Metcalf & Titard**: relationship between component
parts → position and performance. First step = **specify objectives**.

**USERS —** Creditors (cash flow, repayment) · investors (dividends,
growth) · management (internal metrics) · regulators (SEB, standards) ·
others (banks, employees, govt, tax, researchers). SN: finance manager,
top mgmt, trade payables, lenders, investors.

**10 OBJECTIVES —** Profitability · efficiency & managerial
effectiveness · short + long solvency · reasons for change ·
**inter-firm comparison** · forecasts · progress · decision & control ·
**dividend action** · granting credit.

|       | **Comparative**                                            | **Common-size**                                  |
|-------|------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Name  | **Horizontal**                                             | **Vertical**                                     |
| How   | 2+ periods **side by side**                                | Each item as **% of a base**                     |
| Base  | Previous year                                              | BS: total assets / funds = 100 · IS: sales = 100 |
| Shows | Absolute figures, absolute change, %, % change             | Composition; compares **different-size** firms   |
| Types | Comparative BS (solvency) · comparative IS (profitability) | Common-size BS · common-size IS                  |

**FORMULAS (SN) —** Absolute change = CY − PY · % change = Absolute
change ÷ PY × 100 · common-size % = item ÷ base × 100 (building 4 L ÷
funds 16 L = **25%**).

**SN TOOLS (4) —** Comparative · common-size · ratio analysis · trend
analysis (+ cash flow analysis).

> **TRAPS:** *All BS items as % of total = **vertical**, not horizontal
> · vertical is **not** dynamic · QB Q1 false = **A and C** · side by
> side = **comparative statement** · explaining meaning =
> **interpretation** · objective = solvency **and** debt capacity ·
> comparative = horizontal (SN Knowledge Check) · statements help
> forecast (not only history) · useful for small firms too.*
