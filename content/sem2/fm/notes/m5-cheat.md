**FM MODULE 5 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Working Capital Management — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 5 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**MEANING —** Funds for **day-to-day** operations; efficiency +
short-term health; **circulating capital** (SN: also fluctuating,
revolving). Less WC per sale = **higher ROI**. SN: **Gerstenberg** = CA
− CL (net) · **J. S. Mill** = sum of CA (gross).

| **Concept**         | **Remember**                                                                                         |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Balance sheet**   | CA − CL; shows **current solvency**; **ICAI**: net current assets; "WC shortage" = cash scarcity     |
| **Operating cycle** | Inventory procurement → cash collection; purchase, produce, sell; **quicker = less WC, more profit** |
| **Gross WC**        | All current assets; positive WC best shown as CA ÷ CL                                                |
| **Net WC**          | CA − CL; **qualitative**; + or −; negative = distress                                                |
| SN 7 types          | Permanent · regular · negative · temporary (seasonal) · reserve · gross · net                        |

**SIGNIFICANCE (9) —** Solvency · goodwill · easy loans · **cash
discounts** · regular **raw materials** · wages · exploit market · face
crisis · morale.

**IMPROVE (Ebook 6) —** Long-term debt for short-term · inventory mgmt ·
expenses · automation · vendor deals · track performance. SN 5:
inventory, receivables, **GST/ITC automation**, vendor automation,
real-time analytics.

**FACTORS (Ebook 5) —** Nature (manufacturing substantial,
service/trading low) · seasonality · production policy (to order = less)
· market (strong competition = more) · supply (prompt = less). SN 10
adds size, **sales volume** (primary), production & business cycle,
credit terms, credit control (cash sales = less), growth, management,
external.

| **Forecast method** | **Key**                                                     |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Cash forecasting    | Receipts vs disbursements · direct vs indirect              |
| Balance sheet       | Closing cash & bank · = **% of accounts receivable** method |
| Adjusted P&L        | From **opening** cash & bank                                |
| Percent of sales    | Each item as % of sales                                     |
| Operating cycle     | Longer cycle = bigger WC                                    |
| Regression          | Sales vs WC relationship                                    |

**RECEIVABLES —** Debt owed by customers from sales in the **ordinary
course of business**; trade receivable; current asset; receivable days =
collection efficiency. SN: credit policy, collection, **credit analysis
(financial + non-financial)**, discounts (2/10) can erode profit;
strategies conservative / aggressive / moderate. WC estimate: WIP = RM
100% + wages & OH 50%.

> **TRAPS:** *Manufacturing cycle = **time** · rising prices →
> **higher** WC · lower need when met by **current liabilities** · **no
> single** policy fits all · technical insolvency = cut CA, raise CL,
> cut LTD · NWC = CA − CL · very-low CA policy = **vertical** (LMS key)
> · not financed from WC = **accounts receivable** (LMS key) · cash
> sales need **less** WC.*
