**Ratio Analysis — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 3 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**RATIO ANALYSIS —** Ratios from the **same company's** statements;
profitability, liquidity, efficiency, solvency; framework for financial
analysis; enables **cross-sectional** comparison. SN: ratio = relation
of 2 accounting figures; Balance Sheet (positional) + P&L (income); use
over time, across companies, against benchmarks (loan **covenants**).

| **Ratio**                      | **Formula**                                       | **Remember**                                                                          |
|--------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Current**                    | CA ÷ CL                                           | Ideal **2 : 1** · working capital ratio · misleading (assumes liquidation)            |
| **Quick / acid-test / liquid** | (CA − inventory − prepaid/advance tax) ÷ CL       | Ideal **1** · excludes **inventory** · more conservative                              |
| **Cash**                       | (Cash + equivalents + marketable securities) ÷ CL | Strictest; T-bills count; receivables excluded                                        |
| **Debt**                       | Total debt ÷ Total assets                         | **Exclude accounts payable** · \> 1 = more debt than assets · industry-dependent      |
| **ICR** (SN)                   | EBIT ÷ Interest                                   | "Times interest earned" · ≤ **1.5** risky                                             |
| **Gross profit**               | GP ÷ Net sales × 100                              | Before admin expenses · low = poor purchase/sales policy · decline = pricing pressure |
| **Inventory turnover**         | COGS ÷ Avg inventory                              | Avg = (Opening + Closing) ÷ 2 · high = fast sales · low = obsolete risk               |

**FORMULA HELPERS —** COGS = Opening stock + Purchases + Direct expenses
(wages, carriage inwards) − Closing stock · GP = Net sales − COGS · Net
sales = Sales − Returns · salaries are **not** direct.

**LIQUIDITY —** Short-term solvency; used by **creditors / lenders**;
expressed in **pure ratio form**; two basic measures = **current +
quick**. CR much higher than QR = dependence on **inventory**.

**PROFITABILITY —** Ebook: contribution margin, gross profit, net
profit; overall profitability based on **investments**. SN list: GP,
operating, net, ROA, ROE, EPS, ROI, inventory turnover. Turnover ratios
= efficiency / activity / performance (Ebook).

**ADVANTAGES —** Operating/investing/financing efficacy · managerial
efficiency & creditworthiness · projections · weak spots and strengths ·
**SWOT** · benchmarks.

**LIMITS —** **False precision** of accounting data (biggest) · ignores
price-level changes · monetary only · different accounting policies ·
history ≠ forecast.

> **TRAPS:** *CR = 1 → NWC **nil** · paying trade payables **improves**
> CR · CR same, QR down = **higher stock** · leverage = more **debt** to
> raise profit · GP 30,000 on sales 1,20,000 = **25%** · ratio analysis
> helps communication, planning and finding weak spots (A, B, D) ·
> solvency ratio ≠ liquidity · quick ratio ≠ CA/CL · high debt ratio not
> always bad (industry).*
