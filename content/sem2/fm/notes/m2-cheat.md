**Time Value of Money & Capital Budgeting — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Study Guide/Study Notes only*

**TVM —** Money now \> same money later (earning capacity, **time
preference**). SN: due to inflation, opportunity cost, strike price,
money supply. Also called **present discounted value**; DCF analysis =
TVM.

|                         | **Formula**                                 |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| FV single (compounding) | **Amount × (1 + R)^n**                      |
| FV annuity              | Amount × \[(1 + R)^n − 1\] / R              |
| PV single (discounting) | **Amount × 1 / (1 + R)^n**                  |
| PV annuity              | Amount × \[(1 + R)^n − 1\] / \[R(1 + R)^n\] |
| PMT (SN)                | P × r × (1 + r)^n / \[(1 + r)^n − 1\]       |
| Inflation (SN)          | (Price Y2 − Price Y1) / Price Y1 × 100      |

**SN EXTRAS —** 5 components: rate, time, PV, FV, **PMT** · uses:
investments, discounting, loans, retirement, real estate, insurance,
value & volatility (**VIX**; low value = high volatility) · inflation:
CPI (consumer) vs PPI (producer); moderate = healthy, hyper, deflation;
**pensioners worst hit** · factors: panic buying, familiarity /
confirmation / recency bias, loss aversion, demand-supply, production
and labour cost.

**CAPITAL BUDGETING —** Evaluate **large long-term** investments =
**investment appraisal**. Factors (6): technology, demand forecast,
competition, **type of management** (innovative invests more), cash
flow, fiscal/political. Nature: current funds for future benefits,
non-flexible, huge, **irreversible**, strategic. Significance: long-term
commitment, irreversible, large, difficult, profitability. SN steps:
capital mgmt → forecasting → budgeting → analysis → reporting.

| **Technique**     | **Rule**                                           | **Plus**                                                 | **Minus**                                                         |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Payback**       | Outlay ÷ annual inflow; shorter better             | Liquidity, risk, short-run EPS                           | Ignores post-payback flows, timing; **no TVM**; not profitability |
| **ARR**           | Avg income after tax ÷ avg investment              | Simple, accounting data, whole income stream             | **Profits not cash**, no TVM, ignores life                        |
| **IRR**           | Rate where **NPV = 0**                             | TVM, whole life, % return, **no cost of capital needed** | Complex, **multiple/negative rates**, reinvests at IRR            |
| **NPV**           | PV in − PV out at **cost of capital**; \> 0 accept | TVM, all flows, owners' welfare                          | Needs known cost of capital; poor for **unequal sizes**           |
| **PI / BC ratio** | PV inflows ÷ outlay; \> 1 accept, = 1 indifferent  | TVM, less work than IRR, ranks mutually exclusive        | Net PI = gross − 1                                                |

> **TRAPS:** *FV from PV = **compounding** · rate of return of
> investment = **IRR** · DCF = **TVM** · money has time value due to
> **risk-free return possible today** · compare flows by discounting
> **or** compounding · PV \< FV and calculable (both) · SN swaps the
> discounting/compounding formulas (wrong) · QB Q1 key says future value
> "higher than" (concept says lower) · capital budgeting =
> **investment** (not annual) appraisal · payback ignores profits.*
