**MIS MODULE 1 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Management Information Systems — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 1 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**IS —** Ebook: elements that **capture data → information →
disseminate** to decision-makers. SN: **people, processes, data,
technology**. Information = **most critical resource** of MIS. Needs a
**target system**.

**TRIANGLE —** Hardware + software + **liveware**. Input → process →
output → **feedback** (most important, most ignored). Computer literacy
vs **information literacy**.

**WHY IT (3) —** Support business operations · managerial decision
making · strategic competitive advantage.

| **System** | **Level**       | **Key word**                              |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------------------------------|
| TPS        | Operational     | Routine, repetitive transactions          |
| MIS        | Middle managers | Summarised reports from TPS               |
| DSS        | Managers        | Semi-structured / unstructured, scenarios |
| EIS        | Top executives  | Strategic, KPIs                           |
| ERP        | Enterprise      | Integrated finance, HR, production, SCM   |

**DIGITAL CONVERGENCE —** All media and communication **digitized** on a
**single worldwide network** (Ebook/QB). SN: technologies, devices,
industries merge. Information appliance · **disintermediation** = cut
the middleman.

**NETWORK —** "Specific relationship joining a group of **people,
objects, or events**". Juridically independent firms · **IOS** cuts
transaction and co-ordination costs · IT necessary, not sufficient
(McKesson, American Hospital Supply).

**5 COMPONENTS —** IT · structure/culture · management/processes ·
strategy · individuals/roles (equilibrium).

| **Four R's**   | **Meaning**                      |
|----------------|----------------------------------|
| Reframing      | New conception, vision, resolve  |
| Restructuring  | Lean and fit body                |
| Revitalization | Growth by linking to environment |
| Renewal        | **People side + spirit**         |

**5 LEVELS —** 1 localized exploitation · 2 internal integration
(**evolutionary**) · 3 process redesign · 4 network redesign · 5 scope
redefinition (**revolutionary**).

**KNOWLEDGE —** Data workers process; knowledge workers create. OAS +
document imaging (**jukebox**, index server). **KWS**: up to date,
internal consultants, change agents. SN knowledge economy =
**intellectual capital** over tangible assets.

**DATA → INFO → KNOWLEDGE —** 68°F · "NY 68°F at 2 PM" · "unusually warm
for December".

**IP —** Patent 20 yrs · copyright life + 70 (creative works) ·
trademark · trade secret · NDA · DRM · WIPO · DMCA, GDPR.

**APPROACHES —** **Technical** = maths models (CS, management science,
OR) · behavioural · **socio-technical** (de-optimize to fit).

**CHALLENGES —** Strategic business (**long journey to digital firm**) ·
globalization (domestic + foreign) · information architecture · IS
investment · responsibility and control.

**DIGITAL ERA (SN) —** Big data 3 Vs (volume, velocity, **variety**) ·
cloud · BYOD · GDPR/CCPA · AI ethics = **bias and discrimination** ·
accessibility = inclusivity · **digital divide** = unequal access.

> **TRAPS:** *Revitalization ≠ people side (Renewal) · technical
> approach = mathematical · e-procurement streamlines procurement · IoT
> smart shelves = real-time inventory · knowledge economy = intellectual
> capital.*
