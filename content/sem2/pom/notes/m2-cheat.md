**POM MODULE 2 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Product & Service Design — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Quick Notes only (Module 2 "Study Notes"
PDF is FM material)*

**OBJECTIVES —** Main: customer satisfaction, understanding needs.
Secondary: function, cost, profit, quality, appearance, ease of
production, ease of maintenance. 2 of 3 new products fail.

**LEGAL —** Patent (govt grant to inventor) · trademark (word, symbol:
Coke, Apple) · copyright (**form of expression**) · product liability ·
**UCC 1952** (50 US states). Env: EPA after **Stockholm 1972**.

**PLC —** Introduction (low volume, low profit) → growth (exponential,
rivals enter) → **maturity (cut-throat)** → decline. + relative
advantage, compatibility, divisibility, communicability; − complexity,
perceived risk. Works for product forms, not brands.

**TECH LC —** I development (Daimler 1887) · II launch (Model T) · III
growth · IV mature (**process innovation**) · V substitution. Process
growth = product maturity.

**EDISON —** 1,300 inventions, 1,100 patents; lofty goals,
right-to-left, structure, fluidity. Fast to market: **customization**
(one customer) vs **design** (cluster).

**PROCESS —** Ebook 7: clarify task → concept generation (**screening**
kills most) → embodiment → detailed engg (CAD) → physical evaluation →
product & process dev → introduction. QN 4: idea → concept (feasibility)
→ prototype & test → finalisation (specs).

**CONCURRENT ENGG —** Overlapping/parallel phases; 3 teams (program
mgmt, technical, design-build); 5 → 3 years; cross-functional incl.
marketing.

| **Concept**             | **Key**                                                                                      |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Standardisation         | Whitney, Ford; MTS; lower cost, quicker design, delivery, value comparison; less flexibility |
| Mass customisation      | **Extreme postponement**; pull; Dell; 3D printing, CAD                                       |
| Delayed differentiation | **Alderson 1950**; Benetton; inventory shifts **upstream**                                   |
| Commonality / modular   | Common parts; Maruti 800, Omni, Gypsy on one platform                                        |
| Recycling               | **Less** raw material use (QB "maximum" = False)                                             |

**DFM —** Efficient production at **highest quality**; minimise parts,
standard parts, avoid fasteners/tools; submarine 95,000 → 16,000. DFX =
focus on one area.

**SERVICES —** Customer contact = % of time customer is in system. Goods
vs services: inventory, up-front risk, **supply-chain-wide** innovation.
QN: people, **props**, processes; tech helps, not runs; journey mapping;
Airbnb, Spotify, Uber.

**TECH CAPABILITY —** Reverse engg (Sharp-Dyne) · product · process ·
application (nylon tyre cord) · system (fuzzy logic) · core competence
(Hitachi DRAM).

**RELIABILITY —** Measure of design quality; DFR; block diagrams, fault
trees; **redundancy** (2 bulbs); de-rating. **Fault avoidance**
(cheaper) vs **fault tolerance** (redundancy). FMA is ongoing.

**CAD —** Idea generation · augmentation · market testing; virtual wing
tests. GM + IBM, 1960s.

> **TRAPS:** *Maturity, not growth, has cut-throat competition · UCC =
> 1952 · screening kills most ideas · DFM keeps quality high · recycling
> → less raw material · mass customisation built on postponement (True)
> · delayed differentiation simplifies inventory.*
