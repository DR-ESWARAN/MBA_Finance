**POM MODULE 3 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Facility Location Planning — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 3 Ebook + Study Guide + LMS Quick Notes only (Module 3
"Study Notes" PDF is FM material)*

**DEFINE —** Facility location = selecting the site where the plant is
installed. Industrial = **minimise cost**; retail/service = **maximise
revenue**; warehouse = cost + delivery speed. Driver: global economic
boom.

**NATURE —** Long-term; transport can be **25% of selling price**;
location changes total cost by **10%** ("easiest 10% savings"). Costs
locked in once committed.

**WHY (QN 8) —** Expansion · cost savings · resources · extra needs ·
combining businesses · political/social change · increased demand · tax
benefits.

| **Facility**                  | **Top factor**                                          |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Heavy (steel, refinery, auto) | Land/construction cost, raw material, rail, environment |
| Light (TV, pharma, brewery)   | Skilled labour, community, air travel                   |
| Warehouse                     | **Transport & shipping cost**                           |
| Retail & service              | **Proximity to customers**                              |

**4 LEVELS —** Global (site location) → **Macro** (site planning, **most
strategic**) → **Micro** (layout of economic activity centres) →
Sub-micro (workstation). Each phase "frozen" by consensus.

**MFG FACTORS —** Markets · materials · transport (rail = bulk freight)
· labour (**4×** applicants; **50/50** industry-commerce) · other plants
· climate (Katrina) · govt (Doing Business 2006: **India 116/155**).

**SERVICE FACTORS —** Purchasing power · image fit · competition & its
quality · uniqueness · physical qualities · operating policy ·
management quality.

**SITE SELECTION —** Global → country → state → plant. GM Saturn,
**Spring Hill 1985**. India: West/Central preferred, East least. P&G:
**Mandideep** (Ariel, Tide), **Kundaim** (Whisper, Vicks), HP evaluated.

**MACRO —** Master plan · **impact planning** (ecological footprint;
commercial + environmental objectives) · evaluation · layout/space ·
space standards. Master plan: right services, size, location, finance;
**12 areas** incl. **land-use planning**; Phase I gather, Phase II
synthesise ("road map").

**SITE EVALUATION —** **Size** (with expansion room) · utilities ·
transport · land cost (**little importance**, non-recurring). City
(skilled staff, rent) vs industrial estate (tax breaks, no zoning
issues) vs greenfield (dangerous processes, big land, pure water).

**FACTOR RATING —** Factors **1-5**, location scores **1-10**, multiply,
add; highest wins = **Gurgaon** (vs Jammu, Chandigarh). Pair with a
quantitative model.

**CENTRE OF GRAVITY —** **Quantitative**; warehouses; x = Σxw ÷ Σw, y =
Σyw ÷ Σw; assumes cost ∝ distance, fixed points.

**LEAST COST (WEBER) —** Lowest total cost, not max revenue; economic
man, perfect competition; **high material index → raw material**, low →
market; labour deflects.

**QN STEPS —** Strategy: objectives → factors → information → design →
execute. Procedure: assessment (SWOT) → exploration → examination →
final selection.
