**POM MODULE 4 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Capacity Planning — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 4 Ebook + LMS Study Notes Parts 1-2 only*

**CHAIN —** Design → value → market size → volume → **capacity** →
process. Base = **demand**; goal = maximise customer value.

| **Term**                          | **Key**                                                                 |
|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Theoretical (Ebook) / Design (SN) | Ideal, short period, max                                                |
| Normal                            | Average period, normal mix; can exceed 100%                             |
| Effective                         | Given mix, scheduling, maintenance, quality                             |
| Actual (SN)                       | Real output                                                             |
| Efficiency                        | Actual ÷ **effective**                                                  |
| Utilisation (SN)                  | Actual ÷ **design** × 100                                               |
| Rated                             | Capacity × utilisation × efficiency = 100×3×144×0.8×0.9 = **31,104/wk** |

**MEASURE —** Define unit of output first. **Output** = high volume
(Maruti 100,000 avg); **input** = low volume, flexible (machine hours).
Airline seat miles, hospital bed-days, TISCO tonnes/shift.

**7 DETERMINANTS —** Facilities · product/service · process · human ·
operational · supply chain · external.

**PLAN —** Forecast per product → asset array → existing availability.
Know bottlenecks. **Gap analysis** = when and how much. Cushion: 80% use
→ **20%**. Raise only the **bottleneck**. Best level = lowest unit cost.

**STRATEGY —** **Expansionist**: large infrequent, ahead of demand,
**minimises lost sales**, scale + learning (RIL). **Wait-and-see**:
small frequent, lags, overtime/outsourcing, can lose share. Dalmia 5.5 →
12 MT.

**DECISION TREE —** Most common tool; square = decision, circle =
chance; right to left. Mehta: Move **592.5** \> Expand 535 \> Wait
448.5. Processing need = **14,100 h**.

**MAKE OR BUY —** **Proprietary** (headlights) · **standard**
(fasteners) · **specialty** (tyres) · **commodity** (steel; gold, milk).
Core vs non-core (TELCO forge). Strategic · operational ·
organisational. Virtual factory. Not cost alone.

**ALTERNATIVES (6) —** Flexibility · life cycle · systems view · chunks
· smoothing · optimal level. Economies and diseconomies of scale. FMS.

**SERVICES —** No inventory → build for peak + demand management.
Intangibility, customer involvement, perishability. **Apollo**: 72/h,
bottleneck **registration**, doctors 180/h.

**CVP —** Profit = TR − (**TVC + TFC**). Q = (F + π) ÷ (P − V); CMR = (P
− V) ÷ P. Shirts: 11,400 units, Rs 91.2 lakh; CMR 0.625; BEP **11,000 /
Rs 88 lakh**.

> **TRAPS:** *Decision tree is not guesswork (False) · rated capacity
> depends on efficiency (True) · expansionist increases lost sales
> (False) · 100% utilisation not ideal · design capacity not always
> achievable · SN says "MIS" but means capacity management.*
