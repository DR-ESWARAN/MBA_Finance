**SALES MODULE 3 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Sales Operations — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 3 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**SALES OPS —** Key function of any **sales team**; link between
strategy and reps; administers data via **CRM**. Benefits: forecasting ·
growth (3 h/month per rep → 11% faster) · accountability · data
decisions · scalability · new talent.

**BUDGET (Ebook) —** Financial plan allocating **resources** to achieve
forecast sales; objective = control **expenditure of resources**;
planning, coordination, control. Bottom-up (district → region → division
→ central).

**BUDGET (SN) —** Roadmap of **anticipated total revenue** (quantity ×
price); ≠ forecast, ≠ expense budget. Income statement, balance sheet,
cash flow. 7 steps: period → inventory/prices → past data → benchmark →
reps/customers → trends → build.

| **Method**                   | **Who**                        |
|------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Affordable                   | Industrial goods, small firms  |
| Rule of thumb / % of sales   | Fixed % of sales; mass-selling |
| Competitive parity           | Tough competition, large firms |
| Objective & task · Zero base | SG only; ZBB time-consuming    |

**TERRITORY —** Match **opportunity with effort**. Goals incl. control
selling expenses. 12 steps incl. study consumer behaviour, avoid
overlap.

**SIZE —** Small: perishables, high demand/density, rural India,
competition, recession. Large: durables, wholesalers, good transport,
experienced reps, boom.

**ALLOCATION —** Gauss, Whiteman, Bates: optimum size · no duplication ·
equal opportunity · flexible · controllable at **minimum** cost ·
comparable · uniform income · economical · efficient · pair new
salesmen.

**DESIGN —** Control unit → potential → tentative territories. **Market
build-up** (sonography 5,000; Maharashtra 20%) · **Workload (Talley)**:
customers × call frequency.

**SHAPES —** Wedge (urban centre) · circle (**scattered**) · clover leaf
(**random**, a week each, industrial).

**REDISTRICTING —** Still, Cundiff & Govoni 7 steps. Routing + call
frequency = scheduling; time-cushion.

**SN DIVISION —** Geography · population (50k each) · account size ·
niche · product · client name.

**QUOTAS —** Ebook: **direct** (front line) · **overlay** (sum of
directs). SN: revenue · activity · volume · cost-based (20 min) ·
forecast · combination. Tips: build together, not handed down; SPM tech.

**CONTROL —** Standards → measure → compare → correct (critical point
control, management by exception). 9 methods incl. reporting, field trip
every 6 months. SN tools: budget · programs · **audit** · analysis.

**MEETINGS —** Ebook internal: communication + motivation, 5 planning
decisions, costly. SN buyer meeting: **Features → Evidence → Benefits →
Agreement**; client premises · virtual · event · review.

**CONTESTS —** Direct · team · goal. Herzberg (achievement,
recognition); Maslow (esteem, self-actualisation). Toyota/"toy Yoda"
2002.

**EXHIBITION —** Only tool with buyers, sellers, competitors together;
targeted; expensive; industrial info **second only to personal
selling**. Measure cost per lead, cost per order.
