**MKT MODULE 4 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Marketing Channels — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 4 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**DEFINE —** **Marketing channel** = people, organisations, activities
to transfer ownership from production to consumption (= distribution
channel). Right time, place, quantity. Long-term commitment. **Dual
distribution** = more than one channel.

**ROLES (QB: all of above) —** Link producers to buyers · influence
pricing · affect product strategy · customise (install, credit).
Utilities: **time, place, possession** (+ form).

**EFFICIENCY —** **Intermediaries** cut exchange costs; wholesalers can
go, functions cannot. **SCM = long-term partnerships** to cut cost and
redundancy. **Distributor** = manufacturer's representative.

**LEVELS —** Zero (direct: peddling, mail order, own stores, Singer) ·
one (+ retailer; shopping goods) · two (+ wholesaler; 500 → 105
deliveries) · three (+ agent/broker; perishables).

**TYPES (SN) —** Direct = control · indirect = reach. Agents = **no
ownership, commission**. Online + offline = **omnichannel**. Functions:
information (EDI) · promotion · **sorting, grading, packaging (protect +
brand image)**.

**LENGTH —** **Number of intermediaries** (not distance). Short:
perishable, bulky, technical, new, customised. **Long: low unit value**,
standardised.

**DESIGN (6) —** Need → objectives → **specify tasks (QB True)** →
alternatives (levels, intensity, intermediary types) → evaluate
variables (market \[marketing concept, QB True\], product \[bulky: large
lots, few points, QB True\], company, intermediary, environment) →
choose best.

**COVERAGE —** **Intensive** (Parle, everywhere) · **selective** (some)
· **exclusive** (Rolex, few). Intensive ≠ restricted.

**VMS —** **Corporate** (ownership) · **contractual** (franchise) ·
**administered** (dominant power).

**CONFLICT —** Horizontal (same level) · vertical (different levels).
Fix: mediation (no imposed decision) · **arbitration (binding)** ·
**collaborative = mutual benefit, shared goals** · clear agreements ·
communication · negotiation.

**METRICS —** Sales · satisfaction · efficiency (turnover, fulfilment) ·
cost · profitability · **market reach and penetration (geographic
coverage)**. Improve: reviews, **feedback loops (improve performance)**,
tech, adapt, training, **benchmarking (industry standards)**.

**RETAIL —** "To cut a piece". **Connect products with consumers**; give
makers **direct access to end consumers**; **jobs**. India: 2nd after
agriculture, ~11% GDP, 12M outlets, ~2% organised. Ebook functions:
assortment, breaking bulk, holding stock, service. SN: merchandising,
sales, service.

**FORMATS —** Department (one-stop) · **supermarket (everyday needs)** ·
**specialty (niche)** · **e-commerce (digital; broadens reach)** ·
discount. Non-store: direct selling, telemarketing, mail order, TV,
electronic.

**WHOLESALE —** Buy for resale. Merchants (take title) vs agents. **Bulk
breaking = meet specific retailer demands** · **warehousing = store and
manage large stocks**, cuts cost. Relationship → efficiency, inventory,
responsiveness, cost, **risk mitigation**.

> **TRAPS:** *Length ≠ distance · intensive ≠ few outlets · wholesalers
> do not sell to consumers (SN) · bulk breaking = large → small ·
> warehousing lowers cost · QB "wholesaling is one of largest sectors" →
> False (Ebook says retailing) · conflict not always harmful ·
> online/offline not exclusive.*
