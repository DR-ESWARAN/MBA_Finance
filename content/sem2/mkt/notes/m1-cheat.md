**MKT MODULE 1 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Introduction to Marketing — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 1 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**UTILITY —** Want-satisfying power. **Form** (production) · **time** ·
**place** · **ownership** (marketing). Drucker: purpose of business =
**create a customer**.

**DEFINITION —** AMA: planning and executing conception, pricing,
promotion, distribution to create **exchanges**. = satisfaction at a
profit. Essence = **exchange**. Selling: factory → volume; marketing:
marketplace → satisfaction.

**5 ERAS (Ebook) —** Production → Product ("sells itself") → Sales →
Marketing ("consumer is king") → Relationship (retention, trust).
Seller's → buyer's market.

**5 CONCEPTS (SN) —** Production · Product · Selling · Marketing
(customer first) · **Societal** (customer + society). SN dates: sales
1920s-30s, marketing concept 1950s-60s.

**CORE CONCEPTS —** Need (deprivation) → want (shaped by culture) →
**demand** (want + buying power) → product → value → exchange →
**transaction** (unit of measure; barter/monetary) → market (marketplace
/ **marketspace**).

**FUNCTIONS (8) —** Exchange: buy, sell · Physical: transport, store ·
Facilitating: standardise/grade, finance, risk, information. Cost
40-60%.

**PROCESS —** Ebook 6: strategy (long) → planning (2-5 yr) →
programming/allocating/budgeting (1 yr) → implementation →
monitoring/auditing → analysis/research. 5 Cs: customers, company,
competitors, collaborators, context. SN 6: **research first** →
objectives → strategies → tactics → monitor (KPIs) → evaluate (ROI).

|       | **Macro**                                                                             | **Micro**                                                                               |
|-------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| What  | **Broader societal forces**, uncontrollable                                           | **Immediate, controllable** (SN)                                                        |
| Parts | Economic (purchasing power) · socio-cultural · tech · political-legal · environmental | Competitors · suppliers · customers · intermediaries · publics (media, govt, community) |

**ENVIRONMENT (Ebook) —** Scanning = **external** info →
opportunities/threats. Competition: direct · substitutes · all firms.
Cycle: prosperity, recession, depression, recovery. **Inflation**
devalues money · income rise = more sales · **demarketing** in shortages
· consumer rights: choose, informed, heard, safe.

**BUYER —** Lewin B = f(I, P). Interpersonal: culture (broadest), groups
(Asch, reference, opinion leaders), family (most important; autonomic,
husband-, wife-dominant, **syncratic**). Personal: needs/motives,
perception, attitudes, learning, self-concept. 5 steps → **evoked set**
→ **cognitive dissonance**.

**SN BUYING —** **Routine** (low) · **limited** (moderate) ·
**extensive** (high, cars). Buying centre: initiator, influencer,
decider, buyer, user. Org buying = bigger volumes, long relationships.

**STP —** Segments: homogeneous, identifiable, accessible, effective
demand. Bases: geographic (climate), demographic, **psychographic**
(lifestyle, values, personality), behavioural. Targeting:
undifferentiated · differentiated · **concentrated = niche** · micro
(segments of one). Challenge = **overlapping segments**.

**POSITIONING —** Ries & Trout: the mind. Errors: under, over, confused,
doubtful. Identities: who, what, for whom, why me. Milkmaid
repositioning. USP. Repositioning challenge = stakeholder **resistance
to change**.

**MIX —** 4Ps + People (employees, service) · Process · Physical
evidence = 7Ps (services: intangible, inseparable, heterogeneous,
perishable). Price = only revenue element; perceived value guides price.
Skim vs penetration. Push vs pull. 6 Ms.

> **TRAPS:** *Form utility = production, not marketing · income rise →
> **increase** · scanning = external · micro = controllable (SN) vs
> Ebook "uncontrollable" · market research = **first** step · niche =
> concentrated, not micro · branding → loyalty · B2B = personal selling,
> B2C = advertising.*
