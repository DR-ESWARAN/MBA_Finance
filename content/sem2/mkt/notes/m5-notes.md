**1. Promotion and IMC**

- Ebook (QB): **promotion** is a form of **corporate communication**
  that uses various methods to reach a targeted audience with a certain
  message to achieve specific organisational objectives. Needed by
  profit and non-profit firms alike.

- Promotion must fit product, distribution, price and target market.
  Example: a big ad campaign without enough inventory upsets production.

- **IMC (Integrated Marketing Communication)** (QB): a unified strategy
  coordinating many promotional techniques with **one consistent
  message** (salespeople repeat the TV ad's benefits). Ebook definition:
  a continuous effort to plan, execute and evaluate techniques using
  traditional and non-traditional methods. SN: IMC ensures
  **coordination across promotional tools**.

- SN: promotion = strategies to communicate with and influence the
  audience: create **awareness**, foster **interest**, drive **action**;
  builds loyalty beyond the transaction.

**Targets of promotion (Ebook)**

| **Target**                            | **Who**                                                                                                              |
|---------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Members of the target market** (QB) | Current, previous and potential customers; get the most promotional attention                                        |
| **Influencers of the target market**  | News media, special interest groups, **opinion leaders (doctors directing patients)**, trade associations            |
| **Participants in distribution**      | Retailers, wholesalers, distributors; in some industries they take a larger share of the budget than final customers |
| **Other companies**                   | Possible joint ventures, **co-marketing** (shared costs), even competitors                                           |
| **Other stakeholders**                | E.g. industry standard-setting groups                                                                                |

**Features of promotion decisions (Ebook)**

- Know target consumers' media preferences · know beliefs that trigger
  the response · separate tools for specific targets linked to a common
  goal · coordinate advertising, sales promotion, PR · continuous
  information flow. Targeted promotion **decreases advertising
  expenses**.

**Communication process (Ebook)**

- **Source → encoding → transmission → decoding → receipt → response →
  feedback**. Source = information introduced for promotion; feedback
  from consumers is evaluated to change promotion.

> **Source note:** *The Ebook says the process has "eight stages" but
> lists only **seven**. Learn the seven listed.*

**Promotion decision steps (Ebook)**

1.  Set objectives.

2.  Determine the promotion budget.

3.  Target market.

4.  The appeal.

5.  Promotion mix.

- Special pricing strategy: price changed for a short interval to
  promote a product.

**2. Promotional mix**

- Ebook (QB): the **promotional mix** describes a **blend of promotional
  variables chosen by marketers to help a firm reach its goals**; a
  **subset of the marketing mix**; an optimal budget allocation exists.

- SN: promotion mix = combination of tools to engage the audience; the
  **tactical execution** of the promotion decision. Four components:
  **advertising, sales promotion, PR, personal selling**.

| **Element (Ebook)**           | **Definition and examples**                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Advertising** (QB)          | **Paid** presentation of ideas, goods or services by an **identified sponsor** in a **mass medium**: print, radio, TV, billboards, direct mail, catalogues, banner ads, emails                                   |
| **Personal selling**          | Helping and persuading prospects through an **oral presentation**, face-to-face or by phone: sales presentations, meetings, samples, telemarketing                                                               |
| **Sales promotion**           | Media and non-media communication for a **pre-determined limited time** to raise demand or availability: coupons, sweepstakes, contests, samples, rebates, tie-ins, self-liquidating premiums, trade shows, swag |
| **PR / publicity**            | Information carried by a **third party in an indirect way** (QB: "direct way" = **False**): press releases, articles, charity, speeches, seminars; **word of mouth** → influencers, bloggers                     |
| **Direct marketing**          | Channel-agnostic direct communication: SMS, email, websites, fliers, catalogues, letters                                                                                                                         |
| **Corporate image campaigns** | Considered part of the mix (QB: **True**)                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Sponsorship**               | Of an event, contest or race to generate publicity                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Guerrilla marketing**       | Unconventional: graffiti, sticker bombing, flash mobs, viral campaigns                                                                                                                                           |
| **Product placement**         | **Paying a movie studio or TV show** to feature the product (QB: this is NOT "digital marketing" → **False**)                                                                                                    |
| **Digital marketing**         | Marketing via digital technologies: Internet, mobile, display ads                                                                                                                                                |

**3. Advertising**

- Ebook: any **paid** form of **non-personal** presentation by an
  **identified sponsor**. **Advertising** = the process;
  **advertisement** = the message. Identified sponsor + paid media
  distinguish it from **propaganda and publicity**.

- Small firms: someone in sales handles ads; large firms: a separate
  department; most large firms use **outside agencies**.

- **Five major advertising decisions** (Ebook): **objectives → budget →
  message → media → campaign evaluation**.

**Role and goals (SN)**

- Creates **awareness**, builds the brand (familiarity, trust, loyalty).
  Goals: **inform** (features, USP), **persuade** (choose us over
  rivals), **remind** (**reinforce existing brand recall**).

- Strategy: **target audience identification** (demographics,
  psychographics, behaviour) → **message development** (clarity,
  simplicity, **relevance**; emotional) → **creative strategies**
  (visuals, tone, slogan, jingle).

**Media (SN)**

| **Medium**     | **Strength**                                                                   |
|----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Television** | Visual + audio; broad audience; complex messages                               |
| **Radio**      | **Auditory**; reaches people during activities; personal connection; versatile |
| **Print**      | Tangible, enduring; in-depth, detailed information                             |
| **Online**     | Highly **targeted**; real-time engagement metrics                              |

- **Media planning** = when, where, how often ads run → **maximise reach
  and impact**. **Media buying** = negotiating and **purchasing ad space
  or time** within budget.

**Objectives and campaign (SN)**

- Objective categories: **awareness, persuasion, sales** (KC2:
  **True**). **SMART**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant,
  Time-bound.

6.  **Research and planning** (audience, trends, competitors;
    objectives, channels, timeline, budget, KPIs).

7.  **Creative development**.

8.  **Implementation** (timing and frequency).

9.  **Monitoring and optimisation** (track KPIs, adjust).

- Coordination: **IMC**, **cross-channel consistency**, **team
  collaboration**.

**4. Ad effectiveness**

**Ebook**

- Advertisement is an **age-old method** to attract customers (QB:
  **True**); evolved from newspaper and TV to digital. Ad effectiveness
  = whether ads reach the right audience and how successful they are;
  shows strengths, weaknesses, **ROI**.

| **When to study**   | **Purpose**                                                                         |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pre-campaign**    | Ensure the best ad launches (test weeks before; TV ads take a month, banners weeks) |
| **During campaign** | Decide campaign length and further media spend                                      |
| **Post-campaign**   | Did the message and emotional connection land?                                      |

- **Data sources**: from the audience (**online surveys and interviews
  are the best way** — QB: **True**) · digital metrics (**impressions,
  CTR, conversion rate, earned mentions**) · **3rd-party cookies**
  (retargeting, behavioural advertising).

| **Ebook measure**       | **Meaning**                                                                                                    |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Campaign impact**     | **Ad recall** (diagnostic) + awareness, interest, purchase intent, image, favourability (performance measures) |
| **Campaign resonance**  | Ad must be **clear, credible, compelling**                                                                     |
| **Brand fit**           | Ad consistent with brand image (no serious ad for baby apparel)                                                |
| **Brand favourability** | Keeps the brand top of mind; shows what sets it apart                                                          |

**Study Notes**

| **KPI group**  | **Examples**                                          |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| **Awareness**  | Brand recall, aided/unaided awareness, message recall |
| **Engagement** | **CTR, social likes, shares, comments**               |
| **Conversion** | Leads, sales, desired actions                         |
| **ROI**        | Gain vs cost of campaign                              |

- Techniques: surveys and questionnaires · **ad tracking** (real-time
  online) · **A/B testing** (versions to segments → **better
  creatives**) · social media analytics · sales and revenue analysis ·
  **brand lift studies** (before vs after).

- Improve: audience targeting refinement · **creative optimisation** ·
  multi-channel integration · message clarity and simplicity. Adjust on
  metrics: **iterative campaign optimisation** (refine effectiveness) ·
  **budget reallocation** · message refinement · new platforms.

**5. Sales promotion**

- Ebook: persuading potential customers; a **short-term** tactic
  (long-term: **CRM**). Aimed at consumers **and** intermediaries.
  Check: cost vs return, fit with brand image (heavy discounts), lasting
  pull after the campaign.

- SN: short-term incentives to stimulate purchase; urgency, excitement,
  value. Objectives: **boost sales**, **clear inventory**, **build brand
  awareness**, **encourage trial**. KC2: "sales promotions focus on
  long-term loyalty" = **False**.

| **SN type**                  | **Meaning**                                            |
|------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| **Discounts**                | % off, **BOGO**, clearance; bulk-purchase discounts    |
| **Coupons**                  | Redeemable promotional currency (print, digital, app)  |
| **Contests and sweepstakes** | Excitement, buzz, participation                        |
| **Bundling**                 | **Combining products at a discounted rate**; upselling |
| **Loyalty programmes**       | Reward repeat customers; retention                     |

- Ebook consumer sales promotion: **free samples** (launch), **free
  trial** (30-day infomercials), **free gift**. Examples: flash sales,
  BOGO, coupons, giveaways, recurring sales, **tripwires**, limited-time
  offers.

**6. Publicity and PR**

- Ebook: **publicity** = public visibility/awareness of a product.
  **Publicist** does publicity; **PR** = strategy to maintain
  communication with the public. **Publicity stunts** = designed media
  coverage.

- SN: publicity relies on **earned media**, not paid placements (KC2:
  **False** if "paid"). Craft newsworthy stories · engage media outlets
  · use social media.

- PR (SN): strategy for relationships with stakeholders (customers,
  employees, investors, media) · **crisis communication** (transparency,
  timely response) · monitoring (media coverage, sentiment) · community
  engagement (sponsorships, CSR).

**7. Sales force**

- Ebook: the **backbone of marketing**. **Brech**: overall management of
  sales. **AMA**: planning, direction and control of personal selling:
  recruiting, selecting, training, equipping, assigning, rating,
  supervising, paying, motivating. Sales force = the company's
  **personal link to customers**.

| **Ebook: functions of a sales organisation**                | **Ebook: sales manager duties**                     |
|-------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Analyse markets and sales policy; planning and forecasting  | Organise sales and product research                 |
| Decide prices and terms; select, train, control salesforce  | Get best output from the sales force                |
| Sales programmes and promotion; advise on advertising       | Set and control targets, territories, expenses      |
| Allocate territories and targets; customer records; reports | Advise on media and promotion; monitor sales policy |

- Territories by size, transport links, purchasing power, education and
  living standards. Training: **in-house** and **on-the-job**. Evaluate
  on **targets vs actual**.

**8. Common misconceptions (LMS)**

| **Myth**                                | **Fact**                                      |
|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Advertising is solely about selling     | Also awareness, loyalty, narrative            |
| Sales promotion = only discounts        | Contests, bundling, loyalty programmes        |
| Publicity and advertising are the same  | Publicity = earned media; advertising = paid  |
| Sales force only generates revenue      | Insights, relationships, brand representation |
| Ad effectiveness = immediate sales only | Awareness, engagement, ROI too                |
