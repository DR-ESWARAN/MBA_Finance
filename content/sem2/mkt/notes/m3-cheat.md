**MKT MODULE 3 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Pricing Decisions — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 3 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**CORE —** Price = expression of value. **Cost = floor, customer value =
ceiling**, competitors = orienting point (3Cs). Most important
determinant = marketer's objectives. McKinsey: 1% price → **11.1%**
profit.

**COSTS —** Fixed · semi-fixed · variable (airline). High fixed → fill
capacity; high variable → maximise unit contribution. Experience curve
and scale economies justify low prices. Perceived vs potential value.

**FACTORS —** Internal (controllable): **marketing objectives (QB)**,
mix strategy, costs, organisation. External (uncontrollable): market and
demand, competition, economy, resellers, government, society.

**MARKETS —** Pure (uniform commodity, going price) · monopolistic
(range of prices) · **oligopolistic (few sellers, sensitive to each
other)** · monopoly (govt, regulated, non-regulated; demarketing).

**DEMAND —** **Demand curve = price vs demand (True)**. Prestige goods
can slope up. Elastic vs inelastic. Less sensitive: distinctive, no
substitutes, small spend, cost shared, prestige.

**OBJECTIVES —** Ebook: profitability (max, target return) · volume
(sales, share) · meeting competition (value pricing) · prestige. SN:
profit max (balance revenue/cost) · share growth · customer value max ·
**survival = resilience + long-term plan**.

**APPROACHES —** SN: cost-based (fixed + variable) · market-based
(**1st: competitor prices**, then demand-supply) · value-based
(perceived value) · **dynamic (real time, seasonal, up AND down)**.
Ebook: cost-plus/markup, break-even · perceived-value · going-rate,
sealed-bid.

**6 STEPS —** Objective → demand → costs (ABC, target costing) →
competitors → approach → final price (risk-sharing, other parties).

|                | **Skimming**                     | **Penetration**                              |
|----------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Price / target | High → lower; **early adopters** | Low; **price-sensitive**, elastic            |
| Wins           | Fast R&D recovery, exclusivity   | **Quick market share**, scale, deters rivals |
| Risk           | **Attracts competition**         | Short-term profit hit                        |

**MIX PRICING —** Product-line · optional · **captive** (cheap main,
costly supplies; two-part in services) · by-product · bundle.

**DISCOUNTS —** **Cash = prompt payment** · **quantity = large volume**
(QB False trap) · functional/trade · seasonal · trade-in and promotional
allowances.

**ADJUSTMENTS —** Discriminatory (segment, form, location, time) ·
psychological (reference, **odd**) · value · **promotional = temporary
lower price (QB)**: loss-leader, special-event, rebates · geographic:
FOB-origin, uniform delivered, zone, basing-point, freight-absorption ·
international: standard, dual, market-differentiated.

**POLICIES (SN) —** Legal + **antitrust** (no price-fixing, collusion) ·
ethics = **fairness + transparency → trust** · **sustainable =
environmental impact** (sourcing) · adapt periodically to market and
rivals.

> **TRAPS:** *Quantity discount ≠ prompt payment · skimming ≠
> price-sensitive · penetration ≠ high launch price · dynamic ≠ only
> price rises · value-based ≠ only premium · survival ≠ short-term only
> · market-based ≠ competitors only · external factors =
> uncontrollable.*
