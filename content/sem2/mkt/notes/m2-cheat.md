**MKT MODULE 2 — CHEAT SHEET**

**Product Marketing — 1-Page Rapid Revision**

*Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Study Notes only*

**MIX —** 4Ps (McCarthy) + services: People, Physical evidence,
**Process**, Productivity. Advertising, sales promotion, personal
selling, publicity = **promotion**.

**PRODUCT —** Alderson: **bundle of utilities**. Ebook levels: core →
**generic (unbranded)** → branded → differentiated → customised →
**augmented** (unasked extras) → potential (future). SN: core · actual ·
augmented (warranty, service).

**BCG INDUSTRIES —** Volume (few, large) · stalemated (few, small) ·
fragmented (many, small) · specialised (many, high payoff). Stances:
offensive · defensive · niche. Levitt: no such thing as a commodity.

**CLASSIFY —** Non-durable · durable · services. Consumer:
**convenience** (staple, impulse, emergency) · **shopping** (compare) ·
**specialty** (special effort) · **unsought** (life insurance).
Industrial: materials and parts · capital items · supplies and services.

**LINE / MIX —** Item · line (related) · mix (all). **Width** = number
of lines · **depth** = items per line. Too long if dropping raises
profit. Stretch: upward · downward (Parker; risk cannibalisation) ·
two-way (ITC Ashoka).

**CANNIBALISATION —** **New product cuts sales of existing** (Santro →
Santro Xing). OK if new margins are higher.

**EXTEND vs PRUNE (SN) —** Extension = new items in existing line for
**evolving preferences** (cons: dilution, cannibalisation). Pruning =
drop weak items to **focus on high performers**. Balanced mix
**mitigates risk**.

**BRAND —** Landor: a promise · Aaker: mental box, equity · perceptions
in the mind. Trademark = legal protection. Insistence = no substitute.
Aaker 5 levels: switcher → devoted. Types: manufacturer · private label
· **family (one name, related products)** · individual (P&G).

**PACKAGING —** Protection + presentation; differentiation; family
packaging. Labels: dates, composition, storage, use.

**NPD (SN) —** Idea generation → **screening (filter feasible)** →
concept testing (consumer feedback) → design and **prototyping (validate
design)** → test marketing → launch and post-launch. **SCAMPER** =
redesign existing ideas.

**NPD (Ebook) —** Matrix: improved products · line extension · new use ·
**diversification** (highest risk). Koshy: new brands 36% survive; line
ext 71%, brand ext 63%. Concept: need, trust, communicability, usage,
perceived value.

|                | **Intro**                                      | **Growth**      | **Maturity**                  | **Decline**                    |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------|-----------------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Sales / profit | Low / negligible                               | Fast / **peak** | Slow / falling                | Falling / low                  |
| Price          | **Penetration or skim**                        | Competitive     | Adjust, premium or cost       | Cut costs                      |
| Key            | Awareness, trial, **feedback for positioning** | Expand, enhance | **Saturation**, differentiate | Harvest, **phased withdrawal** |

> **TRAPS:** *Generic product = **unbranded** (QB False) · augmented ≠
> tangible only (SN KC key wrong) · family branding ≠ individual ·
> cannibalisation = new hurts old · maturity = saturation, not rapid
> growth · width = lines, depth = items.*
