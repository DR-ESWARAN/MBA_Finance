Principles of Marketing

Module 5 · Promotion Decision

Mind Map · Source: Module 5 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Promotion & IMC

  • Corporate communication → targeted audience → objectives
  • IMC: one message, coordination across tools
  • Targets: target market members · influencers · channel · other firms · stakeholders
  • Steps: objectives → budget → target → appeal → mix
Clinic: IMC is like your clinic website, front desk and discharge leaflet all giving the same post-PCI advice.

2 Promotion Mix

AdvertisingSales promotionPRPersonal selling
Ebook extras: direct marketing · corporate image · sponsorship · guerrilla · product placement (pay movie/TV) · digital

3 Advertising

  • Paid · identified sponsor · mass medium
  • Goals: inform · persuade · remind
  • 5 decisions: objectives → budget → message → media → evaluation
  • Objectives: awareness · persuasion · sales; SMART

4 Media

TVvisual + audio, broad
Radioauditory, personal
Printdetailed, enduring
Onlinetargeted, real time
Planning = reach & impact · buying = purchase ad space

5 Ad Effectiveness

Pre · during · post campaign · surveys & interviews best KPIs Awareness (recall) · engagement (likes, shares, CTR) · conversion · ROI Tools A/B = better creatives · brand lift · ad tracking · iterative optimisation
Trading: A/B testing is like back-testing two strategies on the same data and keeping the winner.

6 Sales Promotion

  • Short-term incentive (long-term = CRM)
  • Boost sales · clear stock · awareness · trial
  • Discounts (BOGO, bulk) · coupons · contests · bundling · loyalty
  • Free samples, free trial, free gift

7 Publicity & PR

  • Publicity = earned media, not paid
  • PR via third party, indirect
  • Newsworthy stories · media relations · social
  • PR: stakeholders · crisis communication · community

8 Sales Force

  • Backbone of marketing; personal link to customers
  • Relationships · revenue · market insights · brand
  • Consultative selling · prospecting · reporting
  • Recruit → train → motivate → measure (conversion, pipeline)
Clinic: a pharma rep explaining a new drug-eluting stent in your cath lab is personal selling.
