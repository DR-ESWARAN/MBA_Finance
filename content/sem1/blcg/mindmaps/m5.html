Membership — Definition & Capacity

  • 1. Subscriber to memorandum
  • 2. Agrees in writing; name in register of members
  • 3. Holds shares; name in depository as beneficial owner

Acquiring & Ceasing Membership

  • 6 Modes of Acquiring:
  • Subscribing memorandum; Allotment (IPO/FPO); Transfer; Transmission; Beneficial owner (depository); Estoppel
  • 10 Ways to Cease:
  • Transfer; Sale under lien; Forfeiture; Surrender; Death; Insolvency; Rescission (misrepresentation/fraud); Winding up; Redemption of preference shares; Issue of share warrants

Borrowing Powers

  • Every trading company has implied power to borrow (unless prohibited by MOA/AOA)
  • Sec 179(3)(c)&(d): Directors pass resolution to borrow; amount must be stated
  • Sec 180: Board restricted from borrowing > paid-up capital + free reserves (except temporary loans)
  • Temporary loans = repayable within 6 months
  • Includes: short-term, cash credit, discounting of bills

Ultra Vires Borrowings

  • Borrowing without authority or beyond Articles limit
  • Securities given = void and inoperative
  • Lender cannot sue the company
  • Consequences:
  • Injunction — restrain company from using borrowed money
  • Subrogation — lender can sue by subrogation for money actually applied
  • Director Liability — personal liability for directors
  • Intra vires borrowing but ultra vires directors = can be ratified by shareholders

Oppression & Mismanagement

  • Oppression (Lord Cooper): "misdemeanour committed by majority shareholders who wrongfully inflict harm on minority shareholders"
  • Mismanagement: affairs conducted prejudicially to public interest or company interest
  • Foss v. Harbottle Rule:
  • Proper plaintiff rule: only company can sue for wrong done to it
  • Majority rule: court won't interfere if wrong can be ratified by majority
  • 4 Exceptions: Ultra vires; Special majority required; Individual rights invasion; Frauds on minority
  • Sec 241: Minority → apply to Tribunal; Sec 245: Class Action

Winding Up

  • "End of life of a company" — permanent closing
  • Liquidator sells assets; pays creditors; distributes balance to shareholders
  • 3 Modes:
  • Winding up by Court — special resolution; default in statutory report; <1 yr commencement; members below min; unable to pay debt; ceases listed
  • Members' Voluntary — directors declare solvent; company can pay debts within 1 year
  • Creditors' Voluntary — company insolvent; creditors involved

Consumer Protection Act

  • CPA 1986 (updated 2019) — simpler, quicker redressal
  • Consumer = buys for self-use (NOT resale/commercial)
  • 6 Rights: Protected from hazardous goods; Informed; Access at competitive prices; Heard; Redressal; Education

Cyber Law & RTI

  • IT Act 2000 — notified 17 October 2000
  • Legal framework for electronic governance; digital signatures; e-commerce
  • Established Cyber Appellate Tribunal
  • First cybercrime recorded = 1820
  • Double jeopardy prohibited — Article 20(2), Indian Constitution
  • RTI Act 2005: openness over secrecy
  • Information Commission = highest authority; can fine non-compliant officials
  • District <5L → State 5–20L → National >20L → Supreme Court
