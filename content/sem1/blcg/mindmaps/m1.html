BLCG Module 1: Corporate Governance

DY Patil MBA Finance | Exam: 15 May 2026

Corporate Governance — Core

  • Rules, processes or laws by which businesses are operated, regulated or controlled
  • Board of directors = responsible for governance framework
  • 5 Common Issues:
  • Conflicts of Interest — personal vs company interest
  • Oversight Issues — board must monitor executives
  • Accountability Issues — checks and balances at every level
  • Transparency — accurate profit/loss reporting
  • Ethics Violations — decisions in best interest of stockholders

Need for Corporate Governance

  • Non-compliance with financial reporting standards
  • Caused huge investor losses
  • Enron (USA) — corporate scandal
  • Satyam (India) — corporate scandal
  • Both fell due to corrupt practices + bad governance

Code of Best Practices

  • Adopted by SEC (capital market) + CAC (companies)
  • Directed mainly at boards of directors
  • Purpose: discipline, transparency, accountability
  • Non-executive directors: max 3 years tenure
  • Shareholders >20% issued capital → must be on board
  • At least 1 director must represent minority shareholders
  • Remuneration committee = mainly non-executive directors
  • Audit committee = independent persons

Board of Directors Role

  • Main role = oversight and planning
  • NOT involved in daily operations
  • Should NOT micromanage
  • May delegate to CEO/CFO in certain circumstances
  • Board committees = subset of full board; provide reports
  • Functions: strategic planning, exec compensation, succession planning, financial integrity, ethics

CSR & Corporate Reporting

  • CSR = self-regulating model; socially accountable to itself, stakeholders, public
  • Also called corporate citizenship
  • Example: Starbucks — 99% ethical coffee, green buildings
  • Social Reporting = community reporting through annual reports
  • Areas: pollution, community, product quality, safety, HR
  • Three purposes: comprehensive view, constraint on irresponsibility, positive motivation

Investor Protection — Companies Act 2013

  • Sec 73: No public deposits accepted (punishable)
  • Sec 34: Criminal liability for misstatement in prospectus (u/s 447)
  • Sec 36: Fraudulent inducement to invest (u/s 447)
  • Sec 123: Dividend credited within 5 days of declaration
  • Sec 125: Investor Education & Protection Fund (unpaid dividends)
  • Sec 136: Member's right to demand Balance Sheet + Auditor Report

Indian Contract Act — Contracts

  • Contract = Agreement + Enforceability
  • 10 Essential Elements:
  • Two parties; Agreement; Consent; Legal intention
  • Contractual capacity (majority age + sound mind + not disqualified)
  • Consideration ("something in return")
  • Free consent (not by fraud/misrepresentation/undue influence/mistake)
  • Lawful object; Certainty; Possibility to perform; Legal formalities
  • Void agreement = Sec 2(g); not enforceable; no legal redress
  • Breach remedy = Sec 73; no compensation for remote/indirect loss

Quasi Contract (Secs 68–72)

  • "Quasi" = pseudo
  • Obligation imposed by law, not by agreement
  • Right is against a specific person (resembles contract)
  • Usually a right to money (liquidated sum)
  • Sec 68: Necessaries supplied to incapable persons
  • Sec 69: Payment by interested person
  • Sec 70: Benefit of non-gratuitous act
  • Sec 71: Responsibility of finder of goods
  • Sec 72: Money paid by mistake
