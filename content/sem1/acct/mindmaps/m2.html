1. What is a Journal?

Definition — L.C. Cropper

"A book employed to classify or sort out transactions in a form convenient for their subsequent entries in Ledger."

Key Identity
JOUR (French) = Day Book of Original Entry Primary Book Day-to-day transactions Chronological order
Journal vs Ledger — Position in Cycle
Transaction → Journal ✅ → Ledger → Trial Balance → Final Accounts
📈 Trading Analogy: Journal = Trade log. Every order execution stamped with time, instrument, quantity, price. You verify the raw log before looking at P&L — same principle here.

4. Opening Entry

What it is

The first entry in a new set of books / new financial year. Records opening balances transferred from previous year.

Rule
ItemTreatmentWhy
All Assets→ DebitAssets carry debit balance
All Liabilities→ CreditLiabilities carry credit balance
Capital→ CreditOwner's equity = credit balance
Key Point
Closing balance (Year N) = Opening balance (Year N+1)
🔀

5. Types of Journal Entry

TypeStructureExample
Simple 1 Debit + 1 Credit (2 accounts only) Cash paid for electricity → Electricity Dr, Cash Cr
Compound >1 Dr and/or >1 Cr (3+ accounts) Buy building (part cash + part loan) → Building Dr; Cash Cr + Notes Payable Cr
Key Distinction
Simple = exactly 2 accounts Compound = 3 or more accounts Both: Total Dr = Total Cr always
Prakash Example — Simple Entry

Jan 1: Started business ₹15,000 → Cash A/c Dr ₹15,000 | To Capital A/c Cr ₹15,000

6. Debit & Credit

MCQ TARGET
AspectDebit (Dr) — LEFTCredit (Cr) — RIGHT
RepresentsReceiver of benefitGiver of benefit
IncreasesAssets, ExpensesLiabilities, Revenue, Equity
DecreasesLiabilities, Revenue, EquityAssets, Expenses
Memory Aid
Dr = DEAD (Debit: Expenses, Assets, Dividends) Cr = CLIC (Credit: Liabilities, Income, Capital)
3 Steps to Find Dr / Cr for Any Transaction
1. Identify 2 accounts → 2. Classify (Personal/Real/Nominal) → 3. Apply Golden Rule
🏆

7. Golden Rules of Debit & Credit

⭐ HIGHEST YIELD
Personal Account
👤
Natural, Artificial, Representative
DEBIT the Receiver
CREDIT the Giver
e.g. Debtor A/c, Bank A/c, Capital A/c
Real Account
🏛️
Tangible & Intangible Assets
DEBIT what Comes In
CREDIT what Goes Out
e.g. Cash A/c, Building A/c, Goodwill A/c
Nominal Account
📊
Expenses, Losses, Income, Gains
DEBIT all Expenses & Losses
CREDIT all Incomes & Gains
e.g. Salary A/c, Rent A/c, Interest Received A/c
🫀 Clinical Analogy: Journal = real-time ECG strip. Every beat (transaction) recorded in sequence. Ledger = Echo report (summary). Golden Rules = ACLS protocol — you must know which drug (Dr or Cr) goes to which account type before acting.
