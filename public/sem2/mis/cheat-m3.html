Module 3 · Electronic Commerce and the Digital Organization

Management Information Systems · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

E-COMMERCE — Exchange via ICTs; electronic trading of goods and electronic material; QB: "systematic and organized network". Greenstein (telecom media) · Turban (buying/selling) · Rayport (technology-mediated) · WTO (production to delivery). ≠ e-business.

MIDDLEMEN — Disintermediation = remove; reintermediation = new ones (escrow, eBay).

Model Meaning · example
B2C Serve end consumers · Amazon
B2B Business to business · largest · Alibaba
C2B Consumer uses online agent · Priceline
C2C Agent between consumers · eBay
P2P PC to PC files/money · Napster, PayPal

EDI — Early 1960s; standard format between businesses; repeat transactions; ANSI X12, EDIFACT; via VANs.

INTRANET — Private, behind firewall; open to outsiders = extranet; event-driven publishing; Ford + suppliers.

TELECOM — Source → encoder → channel (path) → decoder → destination. Bandwidth = data rate (bps); 56 vs 53 Kbps.

PAYMENTS — Dual-slot · dual-chip · one-chip. Initialization = one-time setup; transaction = actual payment.

MEDIA — UTP 100 m, RJ-45 · coax 500 m, Thicknet/Thinnet, BNC. LAN (Ethernet/Xerox) · MAN 5-50 km · WAN · VPN in public net.

SERVICES — HTTPS secure · CDN faster pages · NAT private→public IP · load balancing seamless servers · DNS · DHCP · routers between networks.

DIGITAL ORG — Integration of digital tools · agile, flexible structures · 5G real-time · Slack = informal real-time · UCS integrate channels.

TRAPS: "C2B act as agents between consumers" = False (C2C) · initialization = payment → False · customer reporting online = True · e-commerce and e-business are not interchangeable.
