Management Information Systems

Module 2 · Information Systems in the Enterprise

Mind Map · Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Functional Systems

Marketing · manufacturing (CIM) · HR · accounting · finance e-Business systems = enterprise-wide integration Cross-functional: ERP · CRM · SCM · ECS · EAI

2 TPS

Earliest · cross-functional · routine Internal vs external · batch vs online Cycle 5 activities · 6 processing steps
Trading: the broker's order book logging every buy and sell is a TPS.

3 DSS · MIS · ESS

DSSdata + models, semi/unstructured
MIScollect → disseminate, middle mgmt
ESSsenior mgmt, educated guesses
DSS parts: language · knowledge · problem processing

4 Enterprise Systems

One platform · one repository · customer-driven Challenges: cost up-front, inflexible, daunting SN: phased rollout, training, change culture

5 Competitive Forces

Internet ↑ entrants, buyers, rivalry Least-cost producer · quality control · switching costs IT governance aligns IT with goals

6 Impacts

Transaction cost → firms shrink Agency: nexus of contracts Flatter firms · altered work patterns · ROI = gains vs costs

7 Value Chain

Primary: in, ops, out, marketing, service Support: infra, HR, tech, procurement Tech adds value at each stage · SCM + RFID

8 SIS

Alters strategy and/or structure ROI above industry average = advantage Netflix · Wal-Mart · Delta CRM
Clinical: a STEMI network app that changes how referrals reach your cath lab is an SIS, not a TPS.
