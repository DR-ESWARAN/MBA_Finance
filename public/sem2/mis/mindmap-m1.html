Module 1 · Management Information Systems

Mind Map · Source: Module 1 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Information System

Capture data → information → disseminate to decision-makers People · processes · data · technology Hardware + software + liveware · feedback loop Why IT: operations · decisions · competitive advantage

2 Types of IS

TPSroutine transactions
MISmiddle-manager summaries
DSSsemi-structured, scenarios
EISexecutives, KPIs
ERPintegrates departments
Clinical: TPS = nursing chart · MIS = morning census · DSS = risk calculator before PCI · EIS = board dashboard.

3 Digital Convergence

All media digitized on one worldwide network Information appliance · disintermediation SN: tech + devices + industries merge (telemedicine, Netflix)

4 Networks & IOS

Network = relationship joining people, objects, events IOS cuts transaction + co-ordination costs IT necessary, not sufficient · McKesson

5 Four R's

Reframingnew vision
Restructuringlean and fit
Revitalizationlink to environment
Renewalpeople + spirit
5 levels: 1-2 evolutionary · 3-5 revolutionary

6 Knowledge Economy

Intellectual capital > tangible assets Data → information → knowledge OAS (jukebox) · KWS = consultants, change agents IP: patent 20 yrs · copyright life + 70

7 Approaches

Technical: maths models (CS, MS, OR) Behavioural: human issues Socio-technical: both; de-optimize to fit

8 Challenges

Strategic business · globalization · info architecture · IS investment · responsibility Big data 3 Vs · cloud · BYOD · GDPR AI bias · accessibility · digital divide
Trading: big data velocity is the tick feed; variety is price + news + options chain together.
