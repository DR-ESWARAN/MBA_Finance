Module 2 · Information Systems in the Enterprise

Management Information Systems · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

FUNCTIONS — Accounting · finance · marketing · production · HRM. e-Business systems = IS integrating enterprise-wide processes. Cross-functional: ERP, CRM, SCM; ECS = collaboration; EAI links them.

System Key facts
TPS Earliest; cross-functional; routine; batch or online; internal vs external transactions; data lifeline
DSS Data + models; semi/unstructured; language + knowledge + problem processing systems; supports, not replaces
MIS Collect, process, store, disseminate; middle managers; tech meets business
ESS Tools for senior management; "educated guesses"; external data; drill-down; dashboards

TPS LISTS — Cycle 5: entry · processing · DB maintenance · documents/reports · inquiry. Steps 6: entry · validation · processing/revalidation · storage · output · query.

ROLES — Interpersonal (figurehead, leader, liaison) · informational (monitor, disseminator, spokesperson) · decisional (ESS focus).

FUNCTIONS (Ebook) — Marketing = selling · production = producing · finance = assets and capitalisation · HR = attract, develop, maintain workforce.

ERP BENEFITS — One organisation · firm-wide knowledge management · unified platform · customer-driven. Challenges: daunting, high up-front cost, inflexible, strategic value loss. SN: phased rollout.

5 FORCES + INTERNET — Entrants ↑ · suppliers mixed · buyers ↑ · substitutes (info industries) · rivalry ↑. Harmon 2001 adds employees.

ADVANTAGE — Entry barriers · switching costs · least-cost producer · differentiation · distribution · innovation · quality control (monitor lines).

THEORIES — Transaction cost (firm shrinks) · agency = nexus of contracts (curve down-right) · behavioural (fewer middle managers).

SN — IT governance = align IT with business goals · ROI = gains vs costs · tools: visualisation, predictive analytics, scenario analysis (Tableau, Watson, Power BI) · behavioural impact = altered work patterns and roles.

VALUE CHAIN — Primary: inbound, operations, outbound, sales/marketing, service. Support: infrastructure, HR, technology, procurement. Tech adds value at each stage. SCM + RFID/IoT = real-time tracking.

SIS — Helps alter strategy/structure; supports strategy, changes competition or industry. Advantage = ROI above industry average. Netflix · Wal-Mart · Delta CRM.

TRAPS: DSS level: Ebook says top management; LMS uses semi-structured + scenarios · core competency = unique strength (Apple, Amazon, Toyota) · system analysis = what, design = how.
