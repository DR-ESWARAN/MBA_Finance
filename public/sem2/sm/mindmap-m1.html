1 Strategy

strategos = art of the general · stratus (army) + ago (leading) Links ends (goals) to means (resources) under uncertainty Chandler 1962: goals + action + resource allocation Ohmae: distinguish yourself favourably from rivals
Trading: a trading plan is a strategy: the goal (CAGR), the means (capital, position sizing) and the rules for an uncertain market.

4 Purpose of Business

5 Goals vs Objectives

Goalsbroad, long, not measurable
Objectivesspecific, ≤1 yr, SMART
Objective areas: profit (most important), marketing, productivity, product, social, financial, HR
Clinic: goal = "best cardiac care in the city"; objective = "door-to-balloon under 60 min in 90% of STEMIs this year".

6 Strategic Intent

Hamel & Prahalad, HBR 1989 · obsession with winning Vision → Mission → Business definition → (Model) → Objectives Stretch = resources vs aspirations misfit · leverage · stretch not fit

7 Vision & Mission

8 Core Competence

Prahalad & Hamel HBR 1990 · skills, not assets Tests: many markets · customer benefit · hard to imitate Honda engines · Sony miniaturisation · FedEx logistics · Apple design
Investing: a core competence is the moat you check before buying a stock: can rivals copy it?
