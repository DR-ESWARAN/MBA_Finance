Module 4 · Strategy Formulation & Implementation

Strategic Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

MODES — Entrepreneurial = one powerful person, opportunities · adaptive = reactive · planning = analysts, proactive + reactive.

PROCESS (Ebook) — Objectives → environment (SWOT) → quantitative goals (30%) → divisional plans → performance analysis → selection of strategy (final).

6 STEPS (SN) — Strategic mission (time, core values, business description) → goals → departmental plans → performance analysis (SWOT) → action → revise. SMART, feedback, drafts.

LEVELS — Corporate = what (growth, stability, acquisition, retrenchment) · business = how to compete (SBU) · functional = how to grow, daily actions.

TOOLS — ADL = competitive position (dominant, strong, favourable, tenable, weak) × industry maturity · Hofer = position × life-cycle phase · Shell DPM = sector profitability × competitive capability (refines Boston) · PIMS = SPI Cambridge, 20 yrs, 2,200+ firms, ~100 items.

IMPLEMENTATION — 4th step of SM. Hrebiniak & Joyce 1984. Activities: articulation · validation · communication · monitoring · engagement (ACME). Slow small steps help.

7 stages (SN) 10 failure reasons (SN)
Clear goals → roles → delegate Premature upscaling · poor leadership · no succession plan
Execute & monitor → correct Overwhelming plan · unrealistic goals · structural focus
Closure → retrospective No empowerment · short-term focus · poor communication · weak monitoring

OVERCOME FAILURE — Support network · cash flow · reflect · responsibility · feedback · step-by-step. SG: scenario planning, wargaming, minimise friction.

LEADERSHIP — 6 competencies: anticipate, challenge, interpret, decide, align, learn. 3 classes: production, organisational, strategic. Buy-in needs urgency; implement in phases.

SBU — Profit centre; GE 49 SBUs; SG: scientific grouping of a multi-business firm (P&G, LG). Types: product, market, geography, process, function, hybrid. Not fully independent. Pros: profit, survival, bookkeeping. Cons: costly, rivalry.

QB (outside sources): Owner decides all = simple · two bosses = matrix · dual authority permanent = mature matrix · lower promotions shrink = hourglass · geography = divisional · costly HQ control = divisional · rewards for pay/status = transactional · values and traditions = corporate culture · SBU grouping by function.
