Module 2 · Product Marketing

Principles of Marketing · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

MIX — 4Ps (McCarthy) + services: People, Physical evidence, Process, Productivity. Advertising, sales promotion, personal selling, publicity = promotion.

PRODUCT — Alderson: bundle of utilities. Ebook levels: core → generic (unbranded) → branded → differentiated → customised → augmented (unasked extras) → potential (future). SN: core · actual · augmented (warranty, service).

BCG INDUSTRIES — Volume (few, large) · stalemated (few, small) · fragmented (many, small) · specialised (many, high payoff). Stances: offensive · defensive · niche. Levitt: no such thing as a commodity.

CLASSIFY — Non-durable · durable · services. Consumer: convenience (staple, impulse, emergency) · shopping (compare) · specialty (special effort) · unsought (life insurance). Industrial: materials and parts · capital items · supplies and services.

LINE / MIX — Item · line (related) · mix (all). Width = number of lines · depth = items per line. Too long if dropping raises profit. Stretch: upward · downward (Parker; risk cannibalisation) · two-way (ITC Ashoka).

CANNIBALISATION — New product cuts sales of existing (Santro → Santro Xing). OK if new margins are higher.

EXTEND vs PRUNE (SN) — Extension = new items in existing line for evolving preferences (cons: dilution, cannibalisation). Pruning = drop weak items to focus on high performers. Balanced mix mitigates risk.

BRAND — Landor: a promise · Aaker: mental box, equity · perceptions in the mind. Trademark = legal protection. Insistence = no substitute. Aaker 5 levels: switcher → devoted. Types: manufacturer · private label · family (one name, related products) · individual (P&G).

PACKAGING — Protection + presentation; differentiation; family packaging. Labels: dates, composition, storage, use.

NPD (SN) — Idea generation → screening (filter feasible) → concept testing (consumer feedback) → design and prototyping (validate design) → test marketing → launch and post-launch. SCAMPER = redesign existing ideas.

NPD (Ebook) — Matrix: improved products · line extension · new use · diversification (highest risk). Koshy: new brands 36% survive; line ext 71%, brand ext 63%. Concept: need, trust, communicability, usage, perceived value.
