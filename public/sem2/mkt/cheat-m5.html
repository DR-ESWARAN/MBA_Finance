Module 5 · Promotion Decision

Principles of Marketing · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

PROMOTION — Corporate communication to reach a targeted audience with a message for organisational objectives (QB). Awareness → interest → action. IMC = one consistent message, coordination across tools.

TARGETS — Members of target market (current, previous, potential: QB) · influencers (media, opinion leaders, doctors) · distribution participants · other companies (co-marketing) · other stakeholders.

PROCESS — Source → encoding → transmission → decoding → receipt → response → feedback (Ebook says "eight", lists seven). Steps: objectives → budget → target → appeal → mix.

MIX — Blend of promotional variables (QB); subset of the marketing mix. SN 4: advertising · sales promotion · PR · personal selling. Ebook adds: direct marketing, corporate image (QB True), sponsorship, guerrilla, product placement (pay a movie/TV show; QB: not digital), digital.

ADVERTISING — Paid, identified sponsor, mass medium (QB). Non-personal. Advertising = process; advertisement = message. 5 decisions: objectives → budget → message → media → evaluation. Goals: inform, persuade, remind (= reinforce recall).

MEDIA — TV (visual + audio, broad) · radio (auditory) · print (detailed, enduring) · online (targeted, real-time). Planning = when/where/how often → reach and impact · buying = purchase ad space.

OBJECTIVES — Awareness · persuasion · sales. SMART. Campaign: research and plan → creative → implement → monitor and optimise. IMC, cross-channel consistency, teamwork.

EFFECTIVENESS — Ebook: pre / during / post campaign; surveys and interviews best (QB True); impressions, CTR, conversion, earned mentions; cookies. Measures: impact (ad recall), resonance (clear, credible, compelling), brand fit, favourability. SN KPIs: awareness · engagement (likes, shares, CTR) · conversion · ROI. A/B = better creatives · brand lift · iterative optimisation = refine effectiveness.

SALES PROMOTION — Short-term (long-term = CRM). Boost sales, clear stock, awareness, trial. Discounts (BOGO, bulk) · coupons · contests · bundling (discounted combo) · loyalty. Ebook: free samples, free trial, free gift, tripwires.

PUBLICITY / PR — Earned media, not paid. PR = third party, indirect (QB). Publicist vs PR. PR: stakeholders, crisis communication, monitoring, community.

SALES FORCE — Backbone of marketing (Brech; AMA definition). Relationship building, revenue, market insights, brand. Consultative selling. Training in-house + on-the-job. KPIs: revenue, conversion, retention, pipeline. Personal selling = salesperson demonstrating.

TRAPS: PR is indirect, not direct · product placement ≠ digital marketing · publicity ≠ paid · sales promotion ≠ long-term loyalty · sales force ≠ revenue only · remind ≠ new awareness · media buying ≠ message creation · bulk discount = sales promotion.
