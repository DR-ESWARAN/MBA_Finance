Principles of Marketing

Module 2 · Product Marketing

Mind Map · Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 What is a Product

  • Alderson: bundle of utilities
  • Ebook: core → generic (unbranded) → branded → differentiated → customised → augmented → potential
  • SN: core · actual · augmented (warranty, service)
Clinic: core = relief of angina · actual = the stent and procedure · augmented = follow-up calls and a cardiac rehab plan.

2 Classification

Conveniencestaple · impulse · emergency
Shoppingcompare quality, price
Specialtyspecial effort, brand loyalty
Unsoughtlife insurance, funerals
Industrialmaterials/parts · capital · supplies

3 Line & Mix

  • Width = no. of lines · depth = items per line
  • Stretch: upward · downward · two-way
  • Cannibalisation: new cuts old (Santro Xing)
  • Extension → evolving preferences
  • Pruning → focus on high performers
  • Balanced mix → mitigates risk

4 Branding

Landor: a promise · Aaker: mental box, equity · trademark = legal protection
ManufacturerPrivate labelFamily = one nameIndividual (P&G)
Loyalty Recognition → preference → insistence (no substitute)
Trading: a strong brand is like a blue-chip stock: it holds a premium even when the market is choppy.

5 New Product Development

Ideas → screening (filter) → concept testing (feedback) → prototype (validate) → test marketing → launch & review Techniques SCAMPER (redesign existing) · brainstorming · mind map · crowdsourcing · hackathons Ebook Matrix → diversification = riskiest · Koshy: new brands 36% vs line ext 71%
Clinic: screening → concept test → prototype → test market is like phase I → II → III trials before a full launch.

6 Product Life Cycle

Introlow sales, penetration or skim, awareness + trial
Growthfast sales, peak profit, expand
Maturitysaturation, differentiate, high cash
Declineharvest, phased withdrawal

7 Packaging

  • Protection + presentation
  • Promotes, differentiates, aids storage
  • Labels: dates, composition, use

8 Product Manager

  • Strategy, cross-functional work, go-to-market
  • Whole lifecycle, not only launch
  • Portfolio: focus resources on high performers
