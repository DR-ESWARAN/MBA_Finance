Principles of Marketing

Module 3 · Pricing Decisions

Mind Map · Source: Module 3 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Price = Value

  • Cost = floor · customer value = ceiling · rivals = orienting point (3Cs)
  • Fixed · semi-fixed · variable costs
  • Perceived vs potential value
  • McKinsey: 1% price → 11.1% profit
Clinic: your consultation fee sits between what the clinic costs to run and what patients feel your expertise is worth.

2 Factors

Internal (controllable) Marketing objectives · mix strategy · costs · who sets price External (uncontrollable) Market & demand · competition · economy · resellers · government · society

3 Markets & Demand

Pureuniform commodity, going price
Monopolisticrange of prices
Oligopolyfew sellers, watch each other
Monopolygovt · regulated · free
Demand curve = price vs demand · elastic vs inelastic · prestige goods slope up

4 Objectives

ProfitabilityVolume / shareMeet competitionPrestige
Study Notes Profit max (balance) · share growth · customer value max · survival = resilience + long-term plan

5 Approaches

Cost-basedfixed + variable; cost-plus markup
Market-based1st: rival prices, then demand-supply
Value-basedperceived benefits
Dynamicreal time, seasonal, up & down
Ebook: going-rate · sealed-bid · perceived-value · break-even
Trading: dynamic pricing is like an option premium: it moves with demand (volatility) in real time.

6 New Product Pricing

Skimming High → lower · early adopters · R&D recovery · attracts rivals Penetration Low · quick share · price-sensitive, elastic · scale economies PLC Growth: cut gradually · Maturity: good-better-best · Decline: cut cost or raise price on last units

7 Mix & Adjustments

  • Mix: line · optional · captive · by-product · bundle
  • Cash = prompt pay · quantity = volume · trade · seasonal · allowances
  • Discriminatory · psychological (odd, reference) · value
  • Promotional = temporary low price (loss-leader)
  • Geo: FOB · uniform · zone · basing-point · freight-absorption
  • Intl: standard · dual · market-differentiated

8 Policies & Ethics

  • Legal + antitrust: no price-fixing, collusion
  • Ethics: fairness + transparency → trust
  • Sustainable: environmental impact, sourcing
  • Adapt periodically, differentiate vs rivals
  • DHL case: 17% → ~25% quote conversion
