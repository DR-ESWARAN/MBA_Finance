Principles of Marketing

Module 4 · Marketing Channels

Mind Map · Source: Module 4 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 What is a Channel

  • People, organisations, activities → transfer ownership production → consumption
  • Utilities: time · place · possession (+ form)
  • Intermediaries cut exchange cost; functions cannot be eliminated
  • SCM = long-term partnerships
Clinic: a stent travels maker → distributor → hospital store → cath lab; each link adds time and place utility.

2 Levels & Types

Zerodirect: mail order, own stores
One+ retailer (shopping goods)
Two+ wholesaler (500 → 105)
Three+ agent/broker (perishables)
Direct = control · indirect = reach · online + offline = omnichannel · agents: no ownership

3 Functions

Information (EDI)PromotionSortingGradingPackaging
Packaging = protect + brand image · length = number of intermediaries

4 Channel Design (6 steps)

Need → objectives → tasks → alternatives (levels, intensity, types) → evaluate variables → choose best Variables Market · product (bulky = large lots, few points; low value = longer) · company · intermediary · environment Coverage Intensive (Parle) · selective · exclusive (Rolex)

5 VMS & Conflict

  • VMS: corporate (own) · contractual (franchise) · administered (power)
  • Horizontal = same level · vertical = different levels
  • Mediation · arbitration = binding · collaborative = shared goals · agreements · negotiation
Clinic: a heart-team meeting is collaborative problem-solving; an ethics committee ruling is arbitration.

6 Performance

  • Sales · satisfaction · efficiency · cost · profitability
  • Market reach & penetration = geographic coverage
  • Feedback loops → improve performance
  • Benchmarking → industry standards
Trading: benchmarking a channel is like comparing your portfolio return to the Nifty.

7 Retailers

  • Connect products with consumers; makers get direct access to end consumers; create jobs
  • Dept · supermarket (everyday) · specialty (niche) · e-commerce (digital, wider reach) · discount
  • Ebook: assortment · breaking bulk · stock · service
  • India: ~11% GDP, 12M outlets, ~2% organised

8 Wholesalers

  • Buy for resale; merchants (title) vs agents
  • Bulk breaking → meet specific retailer demands
  • Warehousing → store and manage large stocks
  • Relationship → efficiency, inventory, cost, risk mitigation
