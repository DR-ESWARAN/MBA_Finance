Module 4 · Salesmanship

Sales Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

MEANING — Personal selling is broader; salesmanship never all of it. Stroh: face-to-face influence. Persuade people to want what they already need; needs → wants. Advertising = salesmanship in print. Salesman = hands + head + heart + feet.

DEFINITIONS — Carter: induce to buy · NAMTA: profit to seller + benefit to buyer · Knox: mutual profit · Stephenson: not yet decided · Jagasia: remove ignorance, doubt, suspicion · Holtzclaw: pleasurably, permanently · Blake: buyers' confidence · Sefred Gross: increasing satisfaction.

IMPORTANCE — Connecting link at every step · "eye and ear" of manufacturer · raises turnover, cuts unsold stock.

QUALITIES — Reid 9 (relationships, learn, plan, long-term, communicate, analytical, consistent, persist, morals). Walters/Windate/Russel: personality · product knowledge · customer and buying motives.

SN 11 TYPES — Relational · passive · closer · scripted (brand consistency) · opener · networker · scorekeeper · specialist · communicator · relentless · strategist.

SALES MANAGER — Ebook skills: communication · performance · team leader · personality · decisions · resilience · motivation (hardest) · delegation · passion · positivity. Attributes: integrity · loyalty · availability · listening (most neglected) · learning · innovative · confident · coach · lead by example · strategic. SN 5: optimism · motivational prowess · goal-oriented · corrective action · encouraging success.

PROSPECTING — First step = locate customers. Suspect → prospect (confirmed interest) → customer (spent money). 81.6% top performers 4+ h/day. Referral close 50–70%; 83% happy to refer; follow up after 24 h (21%).

PRE-APPROACH — Preparation for the presentation (not prospecting). Approach = initial contact + small talk.

SN APPROACHES — Instant buddy · guru (facts) · consultant · networker · hard seller.

SELLING TYPES — High-pressure · transactional (gas) · consultative (relationship) · collaborative (partnership).

SEQUENCE — = sales cadence; touchpoints at intervals; SN 5–7 steps; triggers: download, webinar, ad click.

PSYCHOLOGY — Liking = 1 of 6 persuasion principles. 3 min callback +391% · Wed/Thu · 8 attempts, 50% after 5th · local numbers · solve their problem · 2–3 choices (Margalit). Closing: FOMO (loss aversion) · reviews +270% · gestures · don't diss rivals · mystery.

TRAPS: QB: prospecting = preparation → False · three retail types → False · outdoor not retail → False · missionary aim = goodwill · Sefred Gross blank = satisfaction.
