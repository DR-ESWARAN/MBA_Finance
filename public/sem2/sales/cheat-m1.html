Module 1 · Sales Management and Organisation

Sales Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

AMA — Planning, direction and control of personal selling incl. recruiting, selecting, equipping, assigning, routing, supervising, paying, motivating. Forsyth: through the sales team achieve results. SG: segment of the marketing mix.

4 BRANCHES — Top Level Policy Planning (framework of policy) · Line & Staff Operational Planning · Organization · Administration (training, motivation, coordination, execution).

ORDER — Soft-drink driver = order-taker · aluminium siding = order-getter. Sales mgmt = people-oriented.

NATURE (7) — Goal-oriented · continuous · systematic · relationship selling · marketing integration · job positions · pervasive. SN adds boosting sales, distribution, packaging/branding, team, customer relations (12).

KEY ASPECTS — Ebook: marketing mgmt (Eastern Transport, D.A. Stuart intranet) · cultural (Hofstede 5; Korea top-down; NZ travel, Aus salary) · people mgmt · recruitment · motivation (60% capacity; Electrolux; Konica +26%) · leadership · control (formalisation + in India, − in USA) · ethics (Japanese: gifts more unethical). SN: operations · strategy · analysis.

FUNNEL METRICS — Deal volume · average deal size · % successful · sales velocity.

SCOPE — Forecasting · budgeting (expenses) · structure · HRP · hiring · training · objectives · quotas (volume/value) · territories · motivation · compensation · control · packaging feedback · channels · promotion · support · after-sale.

SPECIAL AREA — Geography, isolation, task. Job = communicate, persuade, differentiate. Godin: sales = transfer of emotion.

THEORIES — AIDAS = seller-oriented (A-I-D-A-Satisfaction) · Buying formula = buyer-oriented: product + brand, adequacy + pleasant feeling.

OBJECTIVES — Basic 3: volume, profit, growth. Others: revenue · volume · sustained profit · growth · leadership · prospects→customers (art and science) · motivate · complement marketing functions.

PURPOSES OF ORG — Specialists (division of labour) · all activities done · coordination · define authority (line/staff/functional) · executive time.

7 PRINCIPLES — Span of control (subordinates; narrow = tall) · unity of command (one boss) · stability · centralisation (coordination) vs decentralisation (motivation) · line & staff (staff can't order; MR manager → line) · coordination (two-way) · specialisation.
