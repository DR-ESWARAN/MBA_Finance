Module 2 · Personal Selling

Sales Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

MEANING — Relationship → both parties obtain value. AMA: oral presentation in conversation. Buskirk: contact personally. Cundiff & Still: method of communication. Stanton: personal vs mass. SN: two-way, oral, effective but costly.

NUMBERS — US: 14 million (11%) in selling; world ~100 million.

OBJECTIVES — Qualitative = direction (long-term); quantitative = goals (short-term, time bound; base = sales volume). Ebook 4: volume · profit (mix) · expenses within limits · market share. SG: prospecting, awareness, sales, relationships.

Theory (Ebook 9) Hook
AIDAS Attention (not attitude) … Satisfaction; formula selling; Strong 1925, Sheldon AIDR 1902
Right set of circumstances Situation-response; seller-oriented; dinner example
Buying formula Product + trade name; adequacy + pleasant feeling; every element in presentation
Behavioural equation Howard 1963; drive · cue · response · reinforcement; D = 0 → B = 0
Need satisfaction Identify → fulfil → satisfy; customer-oriented
Consultative Consultant for strategic goals; 8 problems
Relationship-based Service is the link; rapport
Problem-solving Extension of need satisfaction; root of problem
Team selling Complex/key accounts; Boress 2007 ten rules (orchestrator, debrief, sell something)

SN THEORIES — Only 3: AIDAS, right set of circumstances, buying formula. SG: 4 (+ behavioural equation).

CUES — Triggering (start decision, more meaningful) · non-triggering: product cues (colour, size, price) and informational cues (ads, salespeople).

MARKET POTENTIAL — 5 elements: size · growth (5 yrs) · profitability (ROI, ROS, RONA, ROCE) · competition · consumer/product type. Volume = customers × penetration rate; value = volume × average value.

SALES POTENTIAL — Share of market potential a firm expects. Market potential = all players. SN: forecast = outcome of assessing sales potential; alert + motivator.

FORECAST FACTORS — Competition · technology · government action · concern itself. Basis of sales and production budgets.

5 METHODS — Consultative (customer profit, 4 steps) · high octane/creative (prep, incubation, illumination, evaluation, transformation, implementation) · non-manipulative (guilt-free) · compelling/traditional (pressure, not recommended) · cross selling (colleague referral).

SN 7 STRATEGIES — Advantages · functionality · dialogue · advisor · satisfaction · narratives · adjust.

IDEAS — Gap analysis · attribute listing · brainstorming (6-8 people, 1.5 h, no criticism) · forced relationships · morphological (structure) · need identification.

DISTRIBUTION — Direct · indirect · intensive (widest) · selective · exclusive (Bulgari/Harrods; SN Tanishq Delhi).

PRICING — "Standing answer to a recurring question". Objectives incl. penetration (low) and skimming (DuPont). Tripod: cost, demand, competition. Nickerson: cost = resistance point. MR = MC. Govt: ceiling, minimum, dual. SN: value-based, cost-plus, psychological, dynamic.

TRAPS: QB: AIDAS "attitude" = False · first purchase = no assurance of repeat (False) · satisfaction → future buying (True) · team selling solves complex problems (True).
