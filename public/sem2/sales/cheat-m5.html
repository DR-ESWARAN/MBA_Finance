Module 5 · Sales Force Management

Sales Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

MEANING — SFM = sub-system of marketing management; "muscle behind marketing management"; customer-oriented, profit-directed. SN importance: enhanced operations · market understanding · sales strategy.

RECRUIT vs SELECT — Recruitment = search + encourage to apply. Selection = choose most suitable. Salesman = corner-stone (not customer). Sales Manager selects. Challenges: complex products + many channels · competition · career not accepted · no institutions.

EBOOK PROCESS — Quantity → qualities (job description = job, not person; man specification: can he / will he) → sources (9; rivals = unethical) → selection. Order: screening → psychological test → references → medical → final interview → appointment (designation, pay, temp/permanent).

SN PROCESS — Hiring manager requisition → requisition number (HR) → meetings → sources (incl. employee referrals) → resume review → phone pre-screen. Selection: interview → evaluation form → offer → verbal offer → reference/background → job filled.

TRAINING — "Born, not made" → Ebook: born and made. Extent: job difficulty · education · experience · type of buyer. Scheme 7: salesmanship (AIDA = Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) · firm (pride) · product · customers · competitors · day-to-day · self.

ADVANTAGES — Sales volume · lower production cost · early selling maturity (less time per customer) · lower supervision cost · lower turnover · better relations.

Method Hook
Break-in · special assignment Departments · lawyers
Field coaching With an experienced salesman (not alone)
Sales manuals Problems + answers, self-study
Lecture Easiest, quickest, cornerstone, theoretical

Job rotation · follow-up On-the-job · refresher (continuous)

SN TRAINING 7 — Objectives → assess performance → curriculum (expertise, preferences, depth, time, format) → materials → format → release → evaluate (KPIs).

COMPENSATION — "Man is a wanting animal." Pay well: attract · contented · loyalty · relations. Plan: adequate · simple · flexible (new product = commission, regular = salary) · incentive · low admin cost · fair · prompt · promotion. Level lies between company intent and salesforce expectation; plan = compromise.

SUPERVISION — Direction (motivation) + control. Observe, monitor, report · coach · inform · feedback · non-financial motivation. Personal communication best. High morale = high output. SN: too much or too little both harm.

MOTIVATION — Ability ≠ will to work; financial or non-financial; contests, conventions, conferences. SN: profits · team morale · resilience. Not only money.

CONTROL — Reports + records (record = summary) · quota (basis for pay, best measure) · territory (coverage, compare, responsibility, cut cost) · authority · field supervision. Kotler: customers most valuable asset.

EVALUATION — Measure vs objectives · distribute rewards · guide development. Forward-looking, humanistic, MBO.

TRAPS: QB: "Customers" corner-stone → False · AIDA "Interview" → False · field coaching "alone" → False · audio-visual telling + showing → True · morale high → output High · KC: motivation mainly external → False · training one-time → False.
