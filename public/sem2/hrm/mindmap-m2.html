Human Resource Management

Module 2 · Human Resource Planning

Mind Map · Source: Module 2 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Concept of HRP

  • Continuous planning for optimum use of quality employees
  • Best fit people ↔ jobs; avoids shortage / surplus
  • Bridges the gap: org plans ↔ HRM
  • Groundwork for manpower procurement
Dale S. Beach: adequate qualified persons, proper times, satisfaction Decenzo & Robbins: right number & kind, place, time
Clinical: HRP = cath lab rota. Forecast case load, count staff and leavers, then hire, cross-train or adjust shifts.

4 Process of HRP

Ebook: 6 steps
  • 1 Environmental scanning
  • 2 Demand forecasting (quantity + quality)
  • 3 Supply forecasting (internal + external)
  • 4 Matching demand & supply
  • 5 Action plan
  • 6 Monitoring, control & feedback
Study Notes: 8 steps Assess workforce → Scan → Forecast → Skill gaps → Strategies → Implement → Monitor & evaluate → Feedback & adapt
Trap: LMS key says "process begins with forecasting" = True. Demand forecast is not "solely historical".

7 Gap analysis & actions

S = Dbalance
S < Dshortage → hire, train
S > Dsurplus → reduce
Action plans (7)
  • Layoffs · Downsizing · Early retirement · Reduced hours · Hiring
  • Attrition: gradual, leavers not replaced
  • Outplacement: terminate with dignity
Trading: Gap analysis = portfolio rebalancing. Underweight → buy (hire). Overweight → trim (attrition, outplacement).

8 Implement · Evaluate · Traps

Execution: communication · T&D · recruitment & onboarding · downsizing with sensitivity · leadership support · change mgmt Evaluate: goal alignment · workforce capability · cost-effectiveness · employee satisfaction Misconceptions
  • Not just filling vacancies
  • Not one-time: ongoing cycle
  • Not only for large firms
  • Talent = hire and retain
  • HRP bridges, never widens
