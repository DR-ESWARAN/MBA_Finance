Module 4 · Job Analysis & Description

Human Resource Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

CONCEPT — Gathering and analysing information about the content and human requirements of jobs and their context. Shows job families and progression paths.

Author Keyword
Wylie Anatomy of the job
Yoder Job = collection of duties, tasks, responsibilities
Jucius · Flippo Studying operations and duties · collecting info on a specific job
Blum Also the worker's qualifications
Shubin · Scott, Clothier & Spriegel Distinguish each occupation · critically evaluating operations, duties, relationships

9 USES — HR planning (HR inventory, HRIS) · recruitment & selection · training · compensation · appraisal · duties info · health & safety · job re-engineering · counselling.

EBOOK PROCESS (5) — 1 Organisational job analysis (Terry: org charts, flow charts) → 2 Select representative jobs → 3 Collect data → 4 Job description → 5 Job specification (last). Study Notes 5 stages: define purpose → collect → analyse → validate → document.

Method (Ebook 7) Remember
Observation Most reliable first-hand; physical, short-cycle jobs; not for mental work
Interview · Questionnaire Costly, bias, long for managers · many people same job, low cooperation
Checklist Yes/no ticks; costly, large firms only
Critical incidents · Diaries Skilled analyst needed · daily record, beats memory lapse
Technical conference Supervisors as experts; lacks accuracy (job holders absent)

STUDY NOTES 6 METHODS — Interviews (structured = fixed, unstructured = flexible) · questionnaires (closed = quantitative, open = qualitative) · observation · diaries (real-time) · panels (HR + SMEs) · existing documentation.

PROBLEMS (4) — No top-management support · one source only · untrained job holders · distorted data.

JOB DESCRIPTION — Dessler (duties, reporting, conditions) · Pigors & Myers ("pertinent picture") · Ernest Dale 6 writing guidelines · first by-product of job analysis. Components: Ebook 6, Study Notes 8 (title = "initial gateway", summary, duties, reporting, qualifications, conditions, compensation, date & approvals).

JOB SPECIFICATION — Robbins & Coulter: minimum qualifications. Personal · physical · mental · social & psychological. Privacy criticism.

TRAPS: All jobs analysed = False · not concerned with worker qualifications = False · no bearing on HRIS = False · data method = Checklist · relationships section = all of the above · JD ≠ JS but complementary · job analysis is ongoing, not one-time · LMS key says closed-ended questions give "qualitative" insights = True (answer as keyed).
