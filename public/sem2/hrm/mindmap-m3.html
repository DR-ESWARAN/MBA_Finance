Module 3 · Job Design & Its Techniques

Mind Map · Source: Module 3 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Concept of Job Design

  • Contents + methods + relationships of jobs
  • Meets technological/organisational and social/personal needs
  • Cuts dissatisfaction & alienation; non-monetary rewards
  • Follows job analysis
  • Integrates individual needs ↔ org requirements
Armstrong: contents, methods, relationships
Evolution: Industrial Revolution → Ford assembly line & Taylor → Hawthorne (Mayo) → Socio-technical → Herzberg 1960s → Hackman & Oldham 1970s → job crafting, agile

2 Engineering Approach

F.W. Taylor · tasks planned a day ahead · specialisation = hallmark 7 principles
FragmentationTechnologyStandardisation (time & motion)SpecialisationTrainingResponsibilityMonetary incentives
Walker & Guest: 5 problems
  • Repetition · Mechanical pacing
  • No end product · Little social interaction · No input

3 Human Approach (Herzberg)

  • Design jobs around people, not processes
  • Main tool: job enrichment
Motivatorsachievement, work itself, responsibility, growth
Hygieneconditions, policies, salary: absence = dissatisfaction
Clinical: Hygiene = normal K+. Nobody improves because of it, but a low one wrecks everything. Motivators are the therapy.

6 Rotation & Simplification

Job rotation
  • Move across jobs in a group; variety, multi-skill
  • Downside: interruption, alienation, cost
  • Linked to succession planning
Job simplification
  • Split into subparts, no training · Taylor → Ford
  • 5 steps · ECRS: Eliminate, Combine, Rearrange, Simplify

7 Enlargement vs Enrichment

EnlargeHorizontal · more tasks · breadth · boredom ↓ · more supervision · job creep
EnrichVertical · more authority · depth · challenge · self-directed
