Human Resource Management

Module 1 · Introduction & Scope

Mind Map · Source: Module 1 Ebook (LMS)

1 Concept of HRM

  • Managing people with a human approach — "management of man"
  • Binds people + organisation → both goals met
  • Recruit → Select → Train → Develop
  • The "people dimension" of management
Megginson: skills/talents Armstrong: strategic approach Flippo: P-D-C-I-M-S IIPM: people at work
Clinical: HRM = the physiology keeping the organisation's people (cells) healthy & coordinated.

2 Characteristics (6)

  • Universal force & integral part
  • People-oriented
  • Staff function (service to line)
  • Comprehensive (all levels)
  • Line responsibility (own dept)
  • Pervasive responsibility
Hook: "U-P-S-C-L-P"

3 Scope (IIPM 3 + added)

Personnelplanning, recruit, select, train, pay, productivity
Welfarecanteen, crèche, medical, safety, recreation
Industrial Rel.unions, bargaining, grievance, disputes
HR PlanningEmp. Assistance HRISOD (synergy) StaffingTraining & Dev

4 Objectives (10)

  • Procurement · Effective utilisation
  • Desirable relationships · Raise morale
  • Cost containment · Integration of goals
  • Max individual development
  • Satisfy needs + group goals
  • Healthy employer–employee relations
  • Disciplined atmosphere

5 Importance — 4 Levels

Enterpriseattract/retain, develop, cooperation, future team
Professionalquality of work life, teamwork, growth
Socialdignity of labour, jobs↔seekers, health
Nationalskilled manpower → economic growth
Trading: Human capital is the compounding asset — same resources, different people = different returns.

6 Functions

Managerial (P-O-D-C)
  • Planning — forecast HR needs
  • Organizing — structure, authority
  • Directing — the "heart", initiates action
  • Controlling — audit, turnover, morale
Operative (5)
Procurementjob analysis→HRP→recruit→select→place→induct
Developmentappraisal, training, exec dev, career
Compensationjob eval, wage/salary, bonus (Act 1965)
Integrationmotivation, grievance, bargaining, QWL
Maintenancehealth, fringe benefits, PF/pension/gratuity
Trap: Recruitment = attract (positive). Selection = screen & choose (negative).
