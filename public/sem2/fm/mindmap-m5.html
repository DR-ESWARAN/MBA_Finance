Module 5 · Working Capital Management

Mind Map · Source: Module 5 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Working Capital

  • Day-to-day funds · circulating capital
  • Less WC per sale = higher ROI
  • Gerstenberg: CA − CL (net) · J. S. Mill: sum of CA (gross)
Clinic: WC is the blood volume of the business. Too little = shock; too much = congestion.

5 Factors

  • Nature: manufacturing more · utilities less
  • Seasonality · production policy (to order = less)
  • Strong competition = more · prompt supply = less
  • SN: size · sales volume · cycles · credit terms · growth

7 Receivables

  • Debt owed by customers, ordinary course of business
  • Trade receivable · current asset · receivable days
  • SN: credit policy · collection · credit analysis (fin + non-fin) · 2/10 discounts
  • Strategies: conservative · aggressive · moderate
Trading: receivables are unsettled trades. Profit on paper, cash not yet in the account.

8 Exam Traps

  • Manufacturing cycle = time
  • Met via current liabilities → lower WC need
  • No single policy fits all
  • Technical insolvency: ↓CA, ↑CL, ↓LTD
  • Very-low CA policy = vertical (LMS key)
  • Not financed from WC = receivables (LMS key)
