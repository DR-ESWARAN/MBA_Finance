Module 1 · Nature & Scope

Financial Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

DEFINITIONS — Finance = management of flows of money · Howard & Upton: administrative functions, cash and credit · Bonneville & Dewey: raising, providing, managing funds · Guthmann & Dougall: business finance = planning, raising, controlling, administering · Bernstein: acquire, spend, invest, manage risk · Solomon: who/what/where/when/why/how.

FM — Plan, organise, direct, control the investment, financing, dividend decisions. Came out of economics. Used by govt and NGOs too.

5 OBJECTIVES — Supply of funds · returns to shareholders · optimum utilisation · safety · sound capital structure. SN goals: wealth (value) maximisation > profit max · prevent bankruptcy · optimise resources · dividend decisions.

SN EXTRAS — Personal / corporate / public (RBI) finance · economics broader, finance narrower · 4 areas: FM, financial accounting, investment mgmt (= asset mgmt), risk mgmt · finance manager: tactical (advisory) vs strategic; capital management = top duty · Debt: no control, tax-deductible, cheaper · Equity: no EMIs, dilutes control (VC, angels, crowdfunding).

Basis Classification
Period Long > 5 yrs (equity, RE, preference, debentures, FI loans) · Medium 1–5 (bank loans, public deposits, FI loans, lease) · Short ≤ 1 (trade credit, factoring, banks, CP)
Ownership Owner's funds (equity, RE) · Borrowed (debentures, loans, public deposits, lease)
Generation Internal (equity share capital, RE) · External (FIs, banks, preference, deposits, debentures, lease, CP, trade credit, factoring)
Instrument Remember
Equity share Owner, max risk, votes, ownership capital
Preference share Dividend + capital first, fixed rate, votes only at class meetings, redeemable, convertible · cumulative / participating / convertible
Debenture Debt, fixed interest, no votes · secured/unsecured, registered/bearer, convertible/non, first/second
Term loan Regular payments, fixed or floating, established small business
Lease vs HP Lease: 2 parties, rentals, no ownership · HP: instalments, ownership after last instalment
Retained earnings Beginning RE + profit − dividends = Ending RE · zero-cost · prevents dilution
Bond Face/par value · coupon · tenure · credit quality · tradable · YTM annual · zero-coupon at discount · callable helps company, puttable helps investor · indenture
Public deposit Unsecured, ≤ 25% share capital + free reserves, cheaper than bank loan, higher than bank FD rate
CP 1990, unsecured promissory note, 7 days–1 yr (SN: 270 days), Rs 5 lakh, high rating only
CD Banks/FIs, Rs 1 lakh, 7 days–1 yr, discount, demat, transferable
Bank finance > four-fifths of total credit · overdraft · revolving line of credit
Trade credit Most widely used short-term source · net 30/60
Bill discounting Sell bill of exchange before maturity below par · transfers credit risk · cost = discount rate

INTERNAL (SN, 9) — RE · owner's funds · sale of assets · stock sale · reduce working capital · personal loan · sale of investments · discounted sale · delay in salaries (unethical). Risk = power crisis. Appropriated RE = board-earmarked · Unappropriated = Total − Appropriated − Dividends.

TRAPS: Most used short-term source = trade credit · NOT external = retained profits · small firm = bank loan · debenture = long-term, fixed rate · preferred stock has priority · public deposits from the public · RE cause dilution = False · bonds only by govt = False · only shares transfer ownership.
