Module 3 · Ratio Analysis

Mind Map · Source: Module 3 Ebook + LMS Study Notes

1 Ratio Analysis

  • Ratios from the same company's statements
  • Profitability · liquidity · efficiency · solvency
  • Over time · across companies · vs benchmarks (covenants)
  • Balance Sheet (positional) + P&L (income)
Clinic: ratios are the lab panel. Trend and reference range matter more than one value.

2 Liquidity

CurrentCA ÷ CL · ideal 2 : 1
Quick(CA − inventory − prepaid) ÷ CL · ideal 1
Cash(cash + equivalents) ÷ CL · strictest
Pure ratio form · used by creditors · CR "misleading": assumes liquidation, look at cash conversion cycle

3 Leverage & Coverage

Debt ratioTotal debt ÷ total assets · exclude accounts payable
> 1more debt than assets
ICR (SN)EBIT ÷ interest · ≤ 1.5 risky
Leverage = more debt to raise profit (gearing) · industry decides "high"
F&O: leverage is margin. Great in a trend, deadly when rates (volatility) turn.

4 Profitability & Turnover

GP ratioGP ÷ net sales × 100
COGSOpen + purchases + direct exp − close
Inv. turnoverCOGS ÷ avg inventory · avg = (O + C) ÷ 2
Low GP = poor purchase/sales policy · low turnover = excess, obsolete stock

5 Pros & Limits

Advantages Decision efficacy · creditworthiness · projections · weak spots · SWOT · benchmarks Limitations False precision (biggest) · ignores price level · monetary only · differing policies · history ≠ future

6 Exam Traps

  • CR = 1 → working capital nil
  • Improves CR? Pay trade payables
  • CR flat, QR falls → higher stock
  • Sales 1.2 L, GP 30 k → 25%
  • Overall profitability based on investments
  • Quick ratio ≠ CA ÷ CL
