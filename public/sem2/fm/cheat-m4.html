Module 4 · Financial Statement Analysis

Financial Management · Cheat sheet

NotesCheat sheetMind map

STATEMENTS — Formal records of activities and position; often audited. Full set (4): balance sheet · income statement · owner's equity · cash flows. SN: Balance Sheet (position) + P&L (income). Income statement reviews profitability.

5 FEATURES — End of accounting period · facts and opinions · going concern · historical cost · accountants' personal judgement.

ANALYSIS — Critical evaluation for decisions = analysis (methodical classification) + interpretation (meaning, significance). Metcalf & Titard: relationship between component parts → position and performance. First step = specify objectives.

USERS — Creditors (cash flow, repayment) · investors (dividends, growth) · management (internal metrics) · regulators (SEB, standards) · others (banks, employees, govt, tax, researchers). SN: finance manager, top mgmt, trade payables, lenders, investors.

10 OBJECTIVES — Profitability · efficiency & managerial effectiveness · short + long solvency · reasons for change · inter-firm comparison · forecasts · progress · decision & control · dividend action · granting credit.

FORMULAS (SN) — Absolute change = CY − PY · % change = Absolute change ÷ PY × 100 · common-size % = item ÷ base × 100 (building 4 L ÷ funds 16 L = 25%).

SN TOOLS (4) — Comparative · common-size · ratio analysis · trend analysis (+ cash flow analysis).

TRAPS: All BS items as % of total = vertical, not horizontal · vertical is not dynamic · QB Q1 false = A and C · side by side = comparative statement · explaining meaning = interpretation · objective = solvency and debt capacity · comparative = horizontal (SN Knowledge Check) · statements help forecast (not only history) · useful for small firms too.
