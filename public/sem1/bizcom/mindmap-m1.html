2. Delivering Your Message — 5 Keys

"How we communicate is just as important as what we communicate" — Alan Greenspan

1
Be Clear on the Message — Simple, inspiring, easy to relate to
2
Prioritize — Limit facts; use them to support message, not overwhelm
3
Set Context & Expectations — Label the conversation; tell audience what's coming
4
Appearance — Complements the message; directly affects credibility
5
Body Language — Eye contact, facial expression, gestures, posture, movement
🫀 Clinical Analogy: Delivering bad news to a patient = message delivery. It's not just WHAT you say (diagnosis), it's HOW you say it (tone, posture, clarity) that determines how well it's received.

3. Principles of Verbal Communication

Language Has Rules — 3 Types
RuleGoverns
SyntacticStructure, word order, grammar of sentences
SemanticMeaning of words; how to interpret them
ContextualMeaning based on context and social custom
5 Properties of Language
PropertyMeaning
Reality-shapingLanguage determines how we perceive the world
Arbitrary & SymbolicWords have no inherent meaning; humans assign it (Dog = Kutta = Kukur)
Abstract"Amazing beach" vs "hot beach" — abstract loses specificity
Organizes RealityCreates categories: male/female, young/old, tall/short
ObstacleLanguage barriers block communication across regions/departments
Sign Theory (Saussure)
Sign = Signifier + Signified Signifier = Word/Sound Signified = Concept/Image No logical link between them

5. Emphasis Strategies — 4 Types

StrategyWhat it does
Visual CommunicationCharts, diagrams, infographics — make meaning visible; supports, doesn't replace words
SignpostsKeywords guiding listener: "on the other hand", "in conclusion", "for example"
Internal Summaries & ForeshadowingSummaries = what was covered; Foreshadowing = what's coming next
RepetitionDirect = same message; Indirect = alternative ways of saying same point
Key Rule for Visuals

Visuals support your document/presentation — they do not replace it. "A picture may be worth a thousand words, but it is the words that really matter."

🎙️

6. Improving Verbal Communication — 10 Tips

1
Think before you speak — Organise thoughts; eliminate awkward pauses
2
Be clear and concise — Direct language; avoid complex sentences
3
Speak with confidence — Builds trust; command = subject mastery + eye contact
4
Vary vocal tone — Avoid monotone; use inflection for emphasis
5
Be a good listener — As important as being a good speaker
6
Non-verbal cues — Gestures, expressions must align with words
7
Audience perspective — Come down to their level; don't assume expertise
8
Don't rush — Slow down; speak each word clearly
9
Eye contact — Shows confidence, truthfulness, alertness
10
Use their name — Remember and repeat name occasionally in conversation

8. Effective Business Writing — 9 Principles

3 Fs of Tone ⭐ MCQ Target
Formal — Reports Friendly — Emails Familiar — Close colleagues
9 Principles
1
Know your purpose — Clarify goal before writing
2
Know your audience — Expert or layman? Adjust accordingly
3
Keep it simple — Short sentences; short paragraphs
4
Logical structure — One point flows to next
5
Format it — Headings, tables, bullets, bold
6
Get the tone right — Apply the 3 Fs above
7
Plain and simple — Plain English; avoid jargon
8
Spelling & Grammar — Errors signal incompetence
9
Proofread — Print out; read twice (meaning + errors)
