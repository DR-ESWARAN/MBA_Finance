Art vs Science BOTH

🎨 ART
Personal skill
Practical knowledge
Result-oriented
Regular practice
Creativity
🔬 SCIENCE
Systematised knowledge
Continued observation
Universal application
Cause & effect
Validity & predictability
Verdict: Inexact / Social Science — BOTH art & science
Deals with human behaviour → cannot be fully predicted
Teele: "90% art, 10% science → heading to 80:20"
Analogy: Like Cardiology — evidence-based science (RCTs, guidelines) + clinical art (reading the patient, experience).

Functions of Management — POSDC HIGH YIELD ⭐

P
Planning
Deciding in advance — what, when, how. Bridges gap between where we are & where we want to be.
O
Organising
Bringing together physical, financial & human resources. Identifying & grouping activities, assigning duties.
S
Staffing
Right man on right job. Recruitment → Selection → Training → Appraisal → Promotions.
D
Directing
"Life-spark" of enterprise. Supervision + Motivation + Leadership + Communication.
C
Controlling
Measuring performance vs standards → finding deviation → corrective action.
Controlling — 4 Steps (in order)
1. Set Standards 2. Measure Actual 3. Compare → Find Deviation 4. Corrective Action
Also know: Gullick's acronym POSDCORB = Planning, Organising, Staffing, Directing, Co-ordinating, Reporting, Budgeting
🏗️

Levels of Management ⭐

TOP
Top Level
Board of Directors · CEO · MD
Sets objectives & policies · Strategic plans · Appoints middle managers · Accountable to shareholders · Focus: Planning & Coordinating
MID
Middle Level
Branch Managers · Departmental Managers
Executes top-level plans · Trains lower level · Interprets policies downward · Evaluates junior managers · Focus: Organising & Directing
LOW
Lower Level
Supervisors · Foremen · Section Officers
Assigns tasks · Day-to-day instruction · Quality & quantity control · Motivates workers · Image builders · Focus: Directing & Controlling
Analogy: Top = HOD Cardiology | Middle = Senior Consultant | Lower = Resident/Fellow

Mintzberg's 10 Managerial Roles 3 CATEGORIES ⭐

A. Interpersonal — Providing Information & Ideas
👑 Figurehead
Social, ceremonial & legal responsibilities; source of inspiration & authority
🦁 Leader
Provides leadership; manages team/department performance & responsibilities
🔗 Liaison
Networks & communicates with internal & external contacts
B. Informational — Processing Information
📡 Monitor
Seeks information about environment & industry; watches team productivity & wellbeing
📢 Disseminator
Passes useful information to colleagues & team members
🗣️ Spokesperson
Transmits organisational goals & information to the outside world
C. Decisional — Using Information
💡 Entrepreneur
Creates & controls change; generates & implements new ideas
🚨 Disturbance Handler
Manages unexpected roadblocks; mediates disputes
💰 Resource Allocator
Decides best use of funding, staff & organisational resources
🤝 Negotiator
Directs important negotiations within/outside the organisation
