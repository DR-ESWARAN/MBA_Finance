BLCG Module 4: The Companies Act, 2013

DY Patil MBA Finance | Exam: 15 May 2026

Definition of Company

  • Chief Justice Marshall: "artificial, invisible, intangible, existing only in contemplation of law"
  • Lord Lindley: association of persons contributing money to common stock; sharing profits and losses
  • Haney: voluntary association for profit; capital divided into transferable shares
  • Companies Act 2013 replaced Companies Act 1956

9 Characteristics of a Company

  • Incorporated Association — registered under Act; >10 (banking) or >20 (other) = must register or illegal
  • Artificial Legal Person — acts through board; "board = the brains" (Bates v. Standard Land)
  • Separate Legal Entity — independent of members; creditors chase company, not members
  • Perpetual Existence — not affected by death/insolvency of members
  • Common Seal — substitute for signature; 2 directors + secretary present
  • Limited Liability — up to unpaid share value / guarantee amount
  • Transferable Shares — absolute restriction on transfer = ultra vires
  • Separate Property — company owns assets, not individual members
  • Delegated Management — shareholders elect directors; democratic management

Memorandum of Association

  • "Charter of the company" — supreme document
  • Governs relationship with outside world
  • 6 Clauses:
  • Name — unique; add "Limited" / "Pvt Limited"
  • Registered Office — fixes domicile (state)
  • Object — purpose and powers of company
  • Capital — share capital amount and division
  • Liability — members' liability is limited
  • Association/Subscription — subscribers agree to form company; min 7 (public), min 2 (private)
  • Ultra vires MOA = wholly void; cannot be ratified

Articles of Association

  • Rules, regulations, bye-laws for internal affairs
  • Subordinate to MOA
  • Ultra vires AOA = can be ratified by shareholders (if intra vires MOA)
  • Altered by special resolution
  • Public company may adopt Table A in full, exclude it, or partially adopt
  • If Articles don't exclude Table A → Table A automatically applies
  • Contents: shares, transfer, meetings, voting, dividends, accounts, directors, winding up

Certificate of Incorporation & Prospectus

  • "Birth certificate" of company
  • Date in certificate = conclusive evidence (even if wrong)
  • Cannot be cancelled
  • Prospectus = invites public to subscribe securities
  • Only public listed companies can issue
  • Private companies = PROHIBITED from prospectus
  • Key content: 90% min subscription (refund if not met); 10 weeks for allotment/refund; CRISIL credit rating

Shares — Key Sections

  • Sec 44: Shares = movable property; transferable per Articles
  • Sec 45: Each share has a distinctive number (except depository-held)
  • Sec 46: Share certificate under common seal = prima facie evidence of title
  • OPC (One Person Company): introduced by Companies Act 2013; limited liability like sole proprietorship
  • Government Company: ≥51% paid-up capital held by govt; auditors appointed by CAG; annual report to Parliament
