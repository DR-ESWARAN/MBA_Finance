Baumol's Sales Maximisation

Why Managers Prefer Sales over Profit
Manager's salary tied to sales Banks gauge firms by sales Prestige from growing sales HR issues resolved easily
Model Without Advertising
Sales-max output (OD) > Profit-max output (OQ) Price lower under sales-max Must cover minimum profit constraint (PP') OZ exceeds output where TP covers minimum profit
Model With Advertising
Sales-maximiser spends MORE on ads than profit-maximiser Earns higher revenue (DE > QB) Ad spend limited only by profit constraint PP'
Criticisms
Ignores oligopoly interdependence No industry equilibrium shown Ignores potential competition
📊 Trading analogy: Like a broker maximising AUM (assets under management) subject to minimum revenue — not same as maximising own P&L.

5 Fundamental Economic Concepts EXAM HIGH-YIELD

Concept Core Idea Formula / Rule Application
Incremental Accept if ΔRevenue > ΔCost; ignore sunk costs Profit if IR > IC Accept low-price orders when idle capacity exists
Time Perspective Balance short-run gains vs long-run consequences SR: AC ≠ AR | LR: AC = AR Don't accept below-cost orders that damage brand long-term
Opportunity Cost Cost of next best alternative foregone Notional; not in books Pricing, factor remuneration, resource allocation
Discounting (TVM) ₹ today > ₹ tomorrow; future cash flows discounted V = A / (1+i)ⁿ Capital budgeting, investment planning, DCF valuation
Equimarginal Allocate resources so VMP is equal across all uses VMPLA = VMPLB = … = VMPLE Budget allocation, portfolio investment, production mix
🫀 Clinical analogy (Equimarginal): Like allocating cath lab time — marginal benefit per PCI case equalised across patient priority groups (primary PCI vs elective PCI).
📊 Trading analogy (TVM): Exactly how DCF models work in equity valuation — the core of intrinsic value calculation.
