ACCT Module 4: Trial Balance

Definition 4 Objectives Advantages Limitations 2 Formats 2 Methods 2 Types Balancing Rules
📋

1. Definition & Position

Definition (Eric Kohler)

"A list or abstract of the balances or of total debits and total credits of accounts in a ledger, with the purpose being to determine the equality of posted debits and credits."

Also called
Statement of Balance
Key Rule
Dr Total = Cr Total → Arithmetically correct ⚠️ NOT conclusive proof of accuracy
Position in Accounting Cycle
Journal → Ledger → Trial Balance ✅ → Final Accounts
When Prepared
End of financial year (usual) Can be periodic per business need
🎯

2. Objectives of Trial Balance

MCQ TARGET
#ObjectiveDetail
1Check Arithmetical AccuracyDr = Cr proves mathematical correctness of recording
2Locate Accounting ErrorsStarting point for tracing errors in journal/ledger
3Summarise TransactionsGives overall picture of all financial transactions
4Base for Final AccountsP&L and Balance Sheet are prepared from Trial Balance
🫀 Clinical Analogy: Trial Balance = Pre-operative checklist. Confirms everything is accounted for before you proceed to the final diagnosis (Final Accounts). If it fails — stop and find the error.
⚖️

3. Advantages vs Limitations

MCQ TARGET
✅ Advantages
✅ Confirms Dr = Cr for every transaction
✅ Up-to-date account-wise data
✅ Confirms each ledger balance computed correctly
✅ Confirms balances transferred to correct side
✅ Confirms debit/credit columns added correctly
✅ Holistic summary of all ledger accounts
❌ Limitations (Errors NOT disclosed)
Agreement ≠ conclusive proof of accuracy
Only condensed (summary) info per account
Does NOT show profit/loss or financial position
Wrong account error — correct amount, wrong account
Omission error — transaction not recorded at all
Compensating error — two opposite errors cancel out
Error of principle — wrong account type used
📊
