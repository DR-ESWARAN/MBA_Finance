MCQ TARGET
❌ Cash Basis
FeatureDetail
RevenueRecorded when cash received
ExpensesRecorded when cash paid
✅ ProSimple, fewer adjustments, objective
❌ ConIgnores outstanding & prepaid — NOT true P&L
Suited forMostly cash-based businesses

FeatureDetail
RevenueRecognised when earned

✅ ProTrue & fair picture of profit/loss & financial position
❌ ConComplex; many adjustments needed
StandardUsed in formal/corporate accounting

7. Accounting Concepts (9 GAAP Concepts)

HIGH YIELD
#ConceptCore Idea
1Business EntityBusiness ≠ Owner. Owner's capital = liability to business
2Money MeasurementOnly monetary transactions recorded. Non-financial facts excluded
3Dual AspectEvery transaction has 2 effects. Basis of double entry. A = L + C
#ConceptCore Idea
4Going ConcernBusiness assumed to continue indefinitely. Justifies depreciation
5Accounting PeriodLife divided into periods (1 year). Enables periodic reporting
6Cost ConceptAssets at historical cost (purchase + transport + install), NOT market value
#ConceptCore Idea
7MatchingExpenses matched to revenue of same period. Basis of adjustments
8AccrualIncome/expense when earned/incurred — NOT when cash moves
9RealisationRevenue at point of sale — when legal liability to pay arises
🫀 Matching Concept: Cost of a stent attributed to the procedure it was used in — NOT the month it was purchased. Revenue and cost belong to same event.
🏥 Going Concern: Assuming your hospital keeps running when planning 5-year capital expenditure. Without this — no long-term asset valuation.
📜

8. Accounting Conventions

ConventionMeaning
ConsistencySame accounting methods year-on-year for comparability
Conservatism (Prudence)Anticipate losses; do NOT anticipate gains — be cautious
MaterialityDisclose only items significant enough to influence decisions
DisclosureAll relevant information must be disclosed to users
3 Criteria for Accepting a Principle
Usefulness Objectiveness Feasibility
⚠️ Conservatism: Like a cardiologist erring on the side of caution — better to over-investigate than miss a MI. In accounting — recognise losses early, gains only when confirmed.
⚖️

9. Accounting Equation

MCQ TARGET
Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity (Capital)
Owner's Equity = Assets − Liabilities
Why it always balances

All assets are claimed by either owners (equity) or outsiders (liabilities). Total claims = Total resources. Basis of the Balance Sheet and Double-Entry System.

Comes from
Dual Aspect Concept Double-Entry Book-Keeping Luca Pacioli (1494)
