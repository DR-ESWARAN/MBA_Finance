Module 4 · Trial Balance

Principles of Accounting · Notes

NotesCheat sheetMind map
  • Definition (Eric Kohler): "A list or abstract of the balances or of total debits and total credits of accounts in a ledger, with the purpose being to determine the equality of posted debits and credits and to establish a basic summary for final statements"
  • A statement prepared with debit and credit balances of all ledger accounts
  • Also called: Statement of Balance
  • When prepared: Usually at end of financial year; can be prepared periodically
  • Key rule: If Total Debit = Total Credit → arithmetically correct (but NOT conclusive proof)

Position in accounting cycle: Journal → Ledger → Trial Balance → Final Accounts

2. Objectives of Trial Balance (4)

# Objective Detail
1 Check Arithmetical Accuracy Dr = Cr proves mathematical correctness of recording
2 Locate Accounting Errors Starting point for error tracing in journal/ledger
3 Summarise Financial Transactions Gives overall picture of all transactions
4 Base for Final Accounts P&L and Balance Sheet prepared from trial balance

3. Advantages of Trial Balance

  • Confirms identical Dr and Cr for every transaction recorded
  • Up-to-date data — listed account by account
  • Confirms each ledger balance computed correctly
  • Confirms balances transferred to correct (Dr/Cr) side
  • Confirms debit/credit columns added correctly
  • Holistic summary of all ledger accounts in one statement

4. Disadvantages / Limitations (Exam important)

  • Does NOT disclose all errors — trial balance can agree even with errors
  • Agreement is NOT conclusive proof of accuracy
  • Gives only condensed (summary) information per account
  • Does NOT show profit/loss or financial position
  • Cannot detect:
    • Wrong account errors (correct amount, wrong account)
    • Omission errors (transaction not recorded at all)
    • Compensating errors (two opposite errors cancel out)
    • Errors of principle (wrong account type)

5. Preparation of Trial Balance — Steps

  1. Ensure all ledger accounts are balanced (find Dr total − Cr total per account)
  2. Prepare worksheet with: Account Name | Account No. | Debit Balance | Credit Balance
  3. Transfer each ledger balance to correct column
  4. Add up debit and credit columns
  5. If Dr Total = Cr Total → correct | If not → find and rectify the error

6. Formats of Trial Balance

Format Also Known As Structure
Journal Format Vertical Format 4 columns: Particulars, L.F., Debit Balance, Credit Balance
Ledger Format T / Horizontal Format 2 sides: Left (Dr balances) — Right (Cr balances), 3 columns each

What goes on which side (Journal Format):

  • Debit side: Assets, Expenses, Losses
  • Credit side: Liabilities, Income, Gains, Capital

7. Methods of Preparing Trial Balance

Method Process Key Feature
Total Method Accounts NOT balanced; only totalled Grand total Dr = Grand total Cr
Balance Method Closing balances of ledger accounts used More commonly used in practice

8. Types of Trial Balance

Type Description In Use?
Gross Trial Balance Total Dr and Cr of each account shown separately (without balancing) ❌ Not common
Net Trial Balance Only the balance (Dr or Cr) of each account shown ✅ Common practice

9. Balancing of Accounts — Rules

Account Type Balancing Rule Always Shows
Assets Always balanced Debit balance
Liabilities Always balanced Credit balance
Capital Always balanced Credit balance (usually)
Expenses & Revenue NOT balanced — only totalled Dr total = Expense/Loss; Cr total = Income

Carried Down / Brought Down:

  • Excess Dr over Cr → enter "By Balance c/d" on credit side
  • Excess Cr over Dr → enter "To Balance c/d" on debit side
  • Closing Dr balance → brought as "By Balance b/d" (opens on Cr side)
  • Closing Cr balance → brought as "To Balance b/d" (opens on Dr side)

10. Meaning of Balances in Trial Balance

Account Balance Appears In Means
Personal — Dr balance Debit column Debtor (amount due FROM person) = Sundry Debtors
Personal — Cr balance Credit column Creditor (amount due TO person) = Sundry Creditors
Real A/c Always Dr Debit column Asset exists
Nominal — Expenses/Losses Dr Debit column Cost incurred
Nominal — Income/Gains Cr Credit column Income earned

11. Trial Balance vs Balance Sheet

Basis Trial Balance Balance Sheet
Accounts shown All ledger accounts Personal + Real accounts only
When prepared After Ledger After Trading A/c + P&L A/c
Objective Check arithmetical accuracy Show financial position
Order of items Not in any specific order Items shown in proper order
Stock shown Opening stock Closing stock
Headings Debit and Credit Assets and Liabilities

High-Yield Exam Points ⚡

  1. Trial Balance = test of arithmetical accuracy — NOT conclusive proof
  2. 4 Objectives — Check accuracy, Locate errors, Summarise, Base for final accounts
  3. 2 Formats — Journal (Vertical) | Ledger (Horizontal/T)
  4. 2 Methods — Total Method (totals) | Balance Method (closing balances — more common)
  5. 2 Types — Gross (not balanced) | Net (only balances — common)
  6. Assets always Dr | Liabilities always Cr | Expenses Dr | Income Cr
  7. Trial Balance agreement ≠ Error-free books
  8. Personal A/c Dr balance = Debtor (Sundry Debtors) | Cr balance = Creditor (Sundry Creditors)
