1. Subsidiary Books — What & Why

Definition

Books of prime entry where transactions of a particular/similar category are recorded separately. Sub-division of Journal based on nature of transaction.

Also Called
Special Journals Day Books Books of Original Entry Books of Prime Entry
When needed?
Small biz → 1 Journal sufficient Large biz → Journal becomes bulky → Sub-divide
5 Main Types
#BookRecords
1Purchase BookCredit purchases of goods
2Purchase ReturnGoods returned to suppliers
3Sales BookCredit sales of goods
4Sales ReturnGoods returned by customers
5Cash BookAll cash + cheque transactions

2. Needs & Uses

7 Reasons for Subsidiary Books
✅ Saves clerical labourSimilar transactions grouped together
✅ Easier ledger postingSpecific book → specific ledger entries
✅ Internal checkOne clerk's book verified by another
✅ Avoids bulky journalLarge volume split into manageable books
✅ Division of labourDifferent staff → different books
✅ Prompt informationQuick access to category-specific data
✅ Easy error detectionTrial balance disagrees → check relevant book

5. Sales Book & Sales Return Book

Sales Book (Sales Day Book / Sales Journal)
RecordsCredit sales of goods ONLY
Not recordedCash sales → Cash Book | Sale of non-goods → Journal
DocumentOutward Invoice (sent with goods)
ValueNet value = Invoice − Trade Discount
PostingTotal → Sales A/c (Cr); Customer A/c (Dr)
Sales Return Book (Returns Inward Book)
RecordsGoods returned by customers (from credit sales)
Credit NotePrepared by Seller
Debit NotePrepared by Buyer (in acknowledgement)
PostingTotal → Returns Inward A/c (Dr); Customer A/c (Cr)
🎯 Key Rule: Credit Note → Seller | Debit Note → Buyer. Flip it and you're wrong in MCQ.
💵

6. Cash Book — 4 Types

⭐ HIGH YIELD
Definition & Dual Nature
Records all cash + cheque transactions Journal (book of original entry) + Ledger (cash account) = Dual purpose No separate Cash A/c in Ledger needed Left (Dr) = Receipts | Right (Cr) = Payments
1. Single Column (Simple) Cash Book

Cash receipts + cash payments only. 1 amount column each side. Suitable for small traders.

Dr side: "To" | Cr side: "By" | Opening → "To Balance b/d"

2. Double Column Cash Book

Adds Bank column on each side. Bank A/c = Personal A/c (can have Dr or Cr balance).

Overdraft = Cr > Dr in bank → "To Balance c/d" on receipt side

3. Triple Column Cash Book

Adds Discount column on each side. 7 columns per side.

Dr discount = Discount Allowed (expense) | Cr discount = Discount Received (income)

⚠️ Discount column: Totalled NOT balanced (not an account)

4. Petty Cash Book

"Petty" from French "Petit" = small. Maintained by Petty Cashier for small expenses (stationery, postage, refreshments).

Types: Simple | Analytical Columnar
