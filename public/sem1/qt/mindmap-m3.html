QT Module 3: Tabulation

Systematic presentation of classified data in rows and columns for analysis

1. Definition & Objectives

Tuttle's Definition

"Logical listing of related quantitative data in vertical columns and horizontal rows with explanatory words, headings and footnotes to make clear the full meaning of data and their origin."

9 Objectives MCQ
Simplify complex data Highlight characteristics Clarify objective Minimum space Detect errors Facilitate comparison Facilitate reference Identify trends Facilitate statistical analysis
⚖️

2. Classification vs Tabulation

ClassificationTabulation
Prerequisite — comes firstFollows classification
Divides into groups/subgroups based on similarityArranges classified data in rows + columns with headings
Process of groupingProcess of presenting in tabular form
🫀 Clinical Analogy: Classification = sorting patients by diagnosis; Tabulation = entering them into a structured case register with rows + columns.
🗂️

3. Main Parts of a Table 8 — HIGH YIELD

#PartDescription
1Table NumberIdentification; at top of table
2TitleScope + nature — concise, unambiguous
3CaptionsColumn headings — must be self-explanatory
3StubsRow headings — must be self-explanatory
4Main BodyMost important — numerical data; columns top→bottom, rows left→right
#PartDescription
5Ruling & SpacingVertical lines for columns; horizontal at top, bottom, totals only
6Head-noteBelow title — units of measurement (in brackets)
7FootnoteBelow last line — abbreviations, explanatory notes
8SourceRequired for secondary data — indicates data origin
⚠️ Caption = Column heading ⚠️ Stub = Row heading ⚠️ Head-note = units (below title) ⚠️ Footnote = abbreviations (below last line)
📏

4. General Rules for Tabulation 8

1Simple and compact — not overloaded
2In accordance with objective of investigation
3Units of measurement must always be indicated
4Captions and stubs arranged systematically
5Complete and self-explanatory
6Include totals, ratios, percentages where possible
7Entries must be accurate
8Attractive to draw reader attention
